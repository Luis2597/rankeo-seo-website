Charter Guide

Private Jet Charter in Mexico: Complete Guide

Everything you need to know before booking a private jet or helicopter charter with JETCAB in Mexico.

What is included in a JETCAB private jet charter?

Every JETCAB charter includes: dedicated flight crew (pilot + co-pilot, DGAC Mexico certified), in-flight catering personalized to passenger preferences, ground handling at both departure and arrival airports, fuel surcharges, landing and handling fees, all DGAC/AFAC permits for domestic operations, and 24/7 concierge support. International charters additionally include overflight permits, U.S. CBP or foreign customs coordination, and slot reservations at congested airports. There are no hidden fees — the confirmed price is the final price.

How do I book a private jet with JETCAB? How quickly can JETCAB arrange a flight?

Book in 3 steps: (1) Contact JETCAB via WhatsApp (+52 729 108 1200) or the quote form on this page — provide origin, destination, date, and number of passengers. (2) Receive a confirmed, binding price quote within 30 minutes, 24/7, with aircraft options and exact flight time. (3) Sign the digital charter agreement and confirm. For domestic flights, JETCAB can be wheels-up in as little as 2 hours from confirmation. For international routes, 12–24 hours notice ensures full permit processing. Emergency medical evacuations are deployed within 90 minutes.

How much does it cost to charter a private jet in Mexico?

Private jet charter prices in Mexico start from approximately $5,000 USD one-way for short domestic routes (e.g., Mexico City–Querétaro on a Learjet 35) and go up to $120,000+ USD for transatlantic routes (e.g., Mexico City–Madrid on a Gulfstream GV). Pricing factors: aircraft type, route distance, travel date, and availability. JETCAB's three aircraft classes: Learjet 35 (up to 7 pax, from $1,700 USD/hr); Challenger 604 (up to 9 pax, from $2,700 USD/hr); Gulfstream GV (up to 16 pax, from $5,400 USD/hr). All prices include crew, fuel, catering, permits, and airport fees.

What aircraft does JETCAB operate?

JETCAB operates three certified aircraft under its DGAC Mexico Air Operator Certificate (AOC): the Learjet 35 (short-to-mid range, ~2,100 nautical mile range, up to 7 passengers); the Challenger 604 (mid-range, ~4,000 nm range, up to 9 passengers, full lavatory); and the Gulfstream GV (long-range, ~7,400 nm range, up to 16 passengers, private suite). JETCAB also operates an AW139 helicopter and a Bell 206 for city transfers, medical evacuation, and offshore access. All aircraft meet FAA and DGAC airworthiness standards and are insured for $250M+ USD liability.

Can I charter a helicopter with JETCAB? What helicopter does JETCAB operate?

Yes. JETCAB operates an AgustaWestland AW139 (up to 15 passengers, also available in VIP medevac configuration) and a Bell 206 (up to 4 passengers) for executive transfers within greater Mexico City (CDMX), offshore platform access in the Gulf of Mexico, hacienda access, aerial photography, and short-range medical evacuations. Helicopter charter rates start from $800 USD/hr. JETCAB helicopter pilots hold instrument ratings and offshore certifications.

Does JETCAB offer medical evacuation (medevac) flights in Mexico?

Yes. JETCAB operates dedicated aerial medical evacuation (aeroevacuación) services throughout Mexico and internationally. Aircraft are configured with ICU-grade equipment including ventilators, cardiac monitors, defibrillators, and pressurized stretcher systems. Medevac flights are staffed with certified flight nurses and paramedics licensed by SSA Mexico. All three fixed-wing aircraft and the AW139 helicopter are available in medevac configuration. JETCAB coordinates with IMSS, ISSSTE, private hospital networks, and international insurance providers (AXA, MAPFRE, MetLife, Cigna). Emergency deployment within 90 minutes of initial contact.

What airports does JETCAB fly from in Mexico City?

JETCAB primarily operates from Toluca International Airport (AIT/MMTO), 65 km west of CDMX — a dedicated FBO terminal with zero commercial traffic, no security queues, and direct ramp-to-cabin boarding. JETCAB can also arrange departures from Felipe Ángeles International Airport (NLU/MMSM) in northern CDMX. For helicopter transfers, JETCAB uses private helipads in Pedregal, Lomas de Chapultepec, Santa Fe, Polanco, and the CDMX airport helipad network.

What documents do I need for an international private jet flight with JETCAB?

Domestic Mexico flights: a valid government-issued ID (INE, passport, or driver's license) per passenger. International flights to the United States: a valid passport plus a valid U.S. B1/B2 visa (note: ESTA is not accepted for private aviation — a visa is required). For all international destinations: valid passport with 6+ months validity. JETCAB's operations team handles all customs declarations, general declarations (GENDEC), overflight permits, and slot coordination on your behalf. For Cuba, JETCAB manages OCMI permits and Cuban immigration forms.

Is JETCAB available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week?

Yes. JETCAB operates 24/7/365, including holidays. The operations center is staffed around the clock and reachable via WhatsApp (+52 729 108 1200) or the online quote form. Same-night departures are available for domestic routes. For emergency medical evacuations, JETCAB guarantees deployment within 90 minutes of contact. JETCAB has operated without interruption since 1999 and holds DGAC permits for nighttime operations at all key Mexican airports.

How does chartering a private jet compare to flying commercial from Mexico City?

Commercial air travel from CDMX requires arriving 2–3 hours early at a crowded terminal, clearing security and immigration, and waiting at the gate — adding 4–6 hours of non-productive time per round trip. JETCAB charters depart from Toluca's quiet FBO terminal with a 10-minute boarding process: arrive, board, fly. No security theater, no overhead bins, no middle seats. For a group of 6–7 executives, the per-person cost on a Learjet 35 to Monterrey ($9,000 ÷ 7 = ~$1,285 USD) is comparable to a business class commercial ticket — with full productivity, confidentiality, and flexible rescheduling at no penalty.
