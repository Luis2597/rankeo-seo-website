FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

How quickly can JETCAB arrange a private jet?+

Confirmed quote with aircraft, price and route in under 30 minutes. Aircraft ready for departure within 2 hours of booking. Available 365 days a year, 24/7.

How much does a private jet charter in Mexico cost?+

From $1,500 USD/hr for a Learjet 35 up to $5,400 USD/hr for a Gulfstream GV. Helicopter charters from $1,500 USD/hr. All prices include catering, permits and full crew — no hidden fees.

Is JETCAB certified to operate in Mexico?+

Yes. JETCAB holds DGAC certification (Mexico's Dirección General de Aeronáutica Civil), IATA membership and IS-BAO certification — the highest international aviation safety standards. 25+ years of operations without a single incident.

What destinations can JETCAB fly to from Mexico?+

200+ destinations. Domestic: Cancún, Los Cabos, Monterrey, Puerto Vallarta. International: Miami, New York, Los Angeles, Las Vegas, Houston, Dallas, London and beyond.

Can I pay with cryptocurrency?+

Yes. JETCAB accepts wire transfer, credit card and cryptocurrency (Bitcoin and USDC). Discreet payment options are available for high-value bookings.

Does JETCAB offer air ambulance services?+

Yes. JETCAB's air ambulance is a fully equipped flying ICU with specialized medical personnel, available 24/7 with response under 2 hours for medical emergencies anywhere in Mexico.

How long has JETCAB been operating?+

JETCAB has 25+ years of private aviation experience, with over 2,000 flights completed, 500+ satisfied clients and routes to 50+ countries. Our main base of operations is the Aeropuerto Internacional de Toluca (AIT), near Mexico City — offering faster access and fewer delays than the main international airport.
