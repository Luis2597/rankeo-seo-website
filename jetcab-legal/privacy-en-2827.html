Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Effective immediately

I. Who We Are

JETCAB S.A. de C.V. ("JETCAB," "we," "our," or "us") operates private jet and helicopter charter services from Toluca International Airport (AIT), Mexico. We are responsible for the personal data you provide through this website (jetcab.mx) and our concierge channels.

Registered address: Toluca, Estado de México, México. Contact: fly@jetcab.mx · +52 729 108 1200

II. Data We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Full name, email address, WhatsApp / phone number
  • Origin and destination cities, travel dates, passenger count, aircraft preferences
  • Special requests (catering, ground transportation, crew instructions)
  • Payment and billing information (processed by certified third-party processors — we do not store full card numbers)

Information collected automatically

  • Browser type, operating system, device type, IP address
  • Pages visited, time spent, referral source — collected via session cookies and server logs
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level derived from IP)

We use Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) to measure how visitors use the site: pages viewed, time on page, approximate location, device and referral source. Google Analytics sets cookies only when analytics is allowed under your cookie choice (see Section VII). The site also loads Google Tag Manager and a Google Ads conversion tag (Google LLC) to measure whether visits from our ads turn into quote requests. Advertising storage is set to “denied” through Google Consent Mode, so these tags do not place advertising cookies or personalize ads. We do not use Facebook Pixel or Hotjar, and we do not purchase or trade personal data lists.

III. Why We Use Your Data

  • To fulfill your charter request — confirming routes, aircraft, crew, and pricing
  • To communicate flight details — departure times, changes, safety briefings
  • To process payments and issue invoices
  • To send quotes and follow up on pending requests (with your consent)
  • To comply with legal obligations — AFAC (formerly DGAC) flight manifests, SAT invoicing, immigration requirements
  • To improve our service — website statistics (Google Analytics) and route demand analysis

We will never use your data to send marketing materials from third parties or sell access to advertisers.

IV. Confidentiality — Identity Shield

All JETCAB crew members, concierge staff, and ground handlers sign binding Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). Passenger manifests are shared only with regulatory authorities as legally required (AFAC, immigration). Passenger names, routes, and travel dates are never shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

JETCAB does not disclose client identities or travel patterns to the press, to sponsors, or on social media without explicit written permission from the client.

V. Data Sharing

We share your data only when strictly necessary:

  • Aviation authorities (AFAC, formerly DGAC; airport operators) — flight manifests required by law
  • Payment processors — PCI-DSS compliant providers for card transactions
  • Ground transportation providers — only first name and arrival time to coordinate transfers you request
  • IT service providers — website hosting, email and CRM under data processing agreements; quote requests sent through our website are delivered to our team by email
  • Google LLC — website statistics through Google Analytics, only as allowed by your cookie choice
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — if you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, your messages are handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy

Some of these providers (Google, WhatsApp, and our hosting and email providers) may process data outside Mexico, including in the United States, under their own contractual and security safeguards. We also share passenger data with foreign authorities when an international route you request requires it (for example, U.S. Customs eAPIS manifests). We never sell your personal data.

VI. Your Rights (LFPDPPP / GDPR)

Under Mexico’s Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP, published in the Official Gazette on March 20, 2025) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Cancellation / Erasure — request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opposition — object to processing for marketing purposes
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any right, email fly@jetcab.mx with the subject "Data Rights Request." We will respond within 20 business days as required by LFPDPPP.

If you live in California or another U.S. state with a consumer privacy law, you can also ask us what personal information we hold about you and ask us to delete or correct it, using the same email. We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights. JETCAB does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

VII. Cookies

We use the following cookies:

  • jc_cookie_consent (essential, 1 year) — remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies
  • jetcab_lang (essential) — remembers your language (English or Spanish)
  • _ga, _ga_* (analytics, Google Analytics, up to 2 years) — only set when analytics is allowed

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: analytics cookies stay off until you accept them. Elsewhere, analytics is on by default and you can decline it at any time. We do not place advertising cookies: the Google Ads tag runs with advertising storage denied. You can change your choice whenever you like in Cookie settings, or delete cookies from your browser settings.

VIII. Data Retention

We retain charter-related records for 5 years as required by Mexican tax law (SAT) and AFAC flight record regulations. Contact form inquiries that do not result in a booking are deleted after 12 months. You may request earlier deletion by contacting us.
