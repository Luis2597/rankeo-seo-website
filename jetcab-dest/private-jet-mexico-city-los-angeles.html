The trip

Skip LAX and start the day in Beverly Hills

Anyone who has landed at LAX knows the routine: a long taxi, a crowded immigration hall, then an hour on the 405 before you see a palm tree that is not in a parking lot. A private jet to Los Angeles skips the whole thing. JETCAB flies into Van Nuys, in the San Fernando Valley, an airport built for private aviation. You clear US Customs inside the FBO and drive over the hills to Beverly Hills in about 15 minutes.

From there, the city opens up in every direction. A studio meeting in Burbank or Culver City in the afternoon. A late lunch in Santa Monica with the ocean in view. A drive up the Pacific Coast Highway to Malibu at golden hour, with the windows down and the surf breaking on your left. Los Angeles rewards people who can move through it quickly, and landing fifteen minutes from Rodeo Drive gives you a head start on everyone else arriving that day.

At your destination

What to do in Los Angeles after you land

Best time to fly private to Los Angeles

Los Angeles is mild almost all year, which makes it easy to plan. September and October are often the warmest and clearest months, with beach days and long golden evenings. May and June bring the coastal clouds locals call May Gray and June Gloom, which usually burn off by early afternoon. In fall and winter, dry Santa Ana winds can bring heat and high fire risk to the hills. Awards season, from January to March, fills hotels on the Westside and in Beverly Hills, and the Rose Parade in Pasadena opens every new year. For those dates, reserve the aircraft and your hotel early.

Arriving at Van Nuys (VNY) by private jet

JETCAB flies to Van Nuys Airport (VNY), the main private aviation airport for Los Angeles, in the San Fernando Valley. It handles private aircraft only, so there are no terminal lines and no airline crowds. Beverly Hills is about 15 minutes away over the hills, and Burbank's studios, Hollywood and the Westside are within easy reach. US Customs and Border Protection clears international arrivals inside the FBO, using the eAPIS manifest JETCAB files about 24 hours before departure. Your car can wait just outside the private terminal, and your bags go straight from the aircraft to the trunk.

Documents for a private flight to Los Angeles

Every passenger needs a valid passport, and Mexican citizens also need a valid US visa. Travelers of other nationalities need the entry document their passport requires for the United States. JETCAB files the eAPIS manifest with US Customs and Border Protection, so we need passenger names, dates of birth and passport details about 24 hours before departure. Officers check your documents inside the FBO at Van Nuys when you land.

Questions

Los Angeles private jet FAQ

How long is a private jet flight from Mexico City to Los Angeles?

About 3 hours 45 minutes from Toluca International Airport (TLC) to Van Nuys (VNY). Add 45 to 60 minutes by car from Polanco, Santa Fe, Lomas or Interlomas to Toluca, or about 25 by helicopter, and roughly 15 minutes from Van Nuys to Beverly Hills.

How much does it cost to fly private to LA?

From Mexico City, a private jet to Los Angeles starts at $5,200 USD one way on a Learjet 35 for up to 7 passengers, $10,500 on a Challenger 605 for 12 and $19,000 on a Gulfstream GV for 16. Prices are per aircraft and include crew, fuel, catering and permits.

Why fly into Van Nuys instead of LAX?

Van Nuys handles private aircraft only, so there are no terminal lines, and US Customs clears at the FBO. It is about 15 minutes from Beverly Hills and close to the studios, which saves the long drive and the crowded arrivals hall that come with landing at LAX.

What documents do I need to fly private to Los Angeles?

A valid passport for every passenger and, for Mexican citizens, a valid US visa. JETCAB needs passenger details about 24 hours before departure to file the eAPIS manifest with US Customs. Officers check your documents inside the private terminal at Van Nuys when you land.

What is the cheapest way to fly privately to Los Angeles?

The Learjet 35 is the most affordable option, from $5,200 USD one way for up to 7 passengers, which comes to under $750 per seat with a full cabin. Because a private jet to Los Angeles is priced per aircraft, traveling with family or colleagues lowers the cost per person.

Can I continue from Los Angeles to Las Vegas or San Diego?

Yes. You can combine Los Angeles with a few nights in Las Vegas or a stop in San Diego before flying home to Mexico City. Tell us the full itinerary when you ask for your quote, and every leg is priced and confirmed within 30 minutes, per aircraft.
