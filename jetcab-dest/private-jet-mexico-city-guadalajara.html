The trip

The most Mexican of Mexican cities

Guadalajara gave the world mariachi and tequila, and it wears both proudly. The historic center is built of warm stone around the twin yellow spires of the cathedral, with four plazas laid out around it in the shape of a cross. Inside the Hospicio Cabañas, José Clemente Orozco painted a man wrapped in flames across the chapel dome, a work many people consider the finest mural in the whole country.

The modern city is just as compelling. Zapopan and Puerta de Hierro hold the corporate offices and tech companies that earned Guadalajara the nickname Mexico's Silicon Valley, while Colonia Americana is the neighborhood of independent coffee bars, design shops and some of the most talked-about kitchens in Mexico. An hour away, the blue agave fields of Tequila climb toward the volcano, and to the south lies Lake Chapala, the largest freshwater lake in Mexico.

At your destination

What to see in Guadalajara between meetings

Best time to fly to Guadalajara

Guadalajara has one of the most comfortable climates in Mexico, so a private jet to Guadalajara works in any month. The dry season from November to May brings sunny days and cool nights, and in March the jacarandas turn the avenues purple. April and May are the warmest months. The rainy season, June to September, usually means a short, heavy storm in the afternoon. October is festival month with the Fiestas de Octubre, and from late November into early December the Guadalajara International Book Fair, the largest Spanish-language book fair in the world, fills every hotel. Confirm early for those dates.

Arriving at Guadalajara International (GDL)

Guadalajara International (GDL), officially Miguel Hidalgo y Costilla International, is 530 km from Toluca and sits about 20 km south of the city center on the road to Lake Chapala. Private aircraft are handled in the general aviation area, apart from the busy commercial terminal. The historic center is about 30 minutes away, Colonia Americana and Providencia around 35, Zapopan and Andares about 45, Lake Chapala 30 and Tequila about 1 hour 15 minutes. This is a domestic flight, so there are no customs formalities on arrival.

Documents for a private flight to Guadalajara

Your flight to Guadalajara is domestic, so Mexican nationals only need a valid official photo ID, such as an INE card or a passport. Foreign visitors should carry the passport they used to enter Mexico. Send the full name of every passenger on WhatsApp when you confirm, and the aircraft can be ready about 2 hours later. Customs and immigration do not apply on this route.

How long is a private jet from Mexico City to Guadalajara?

About 50 minutes in the air from Toluca (AIT) to Guadalajara International (GDL), the fastest connection between Mexico's two largest cities. Add about an hour by car from Polanco to Toluca and under 10 minutes from the curb to the aircraft steps.

Yes. A JETCAB private jet flies nonstop from Toluca to Guadalajara International in about 50 minutes. Commercial airlines also fly direct from Mexico City, but once you add check-in, security, boarding and baggage claim, the commercial door-to-door trip usually stretches past two hours.

Yes, it is one of the most popular same-day routes JETCAB flies. Leave Toluca at eight, land at nine, hold your meetings in Zapopan or the historic center, and fly home in the late afternoon. The aircraft is ready for the return whenever your day ends.
