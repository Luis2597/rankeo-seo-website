The trip

The Caribbean before lunch

The flight east crosses the whole country. You lift off from Toluca beside the pine slopes of the Nevado, cruise out over the Gulf of Mexico, and start your descent as the water below turns from navy to an almost electric turquoise. That color comes from the Mesoamerican Reef, the second-longest barrier reef on the planet, which runs from here down the coast toward Belize. When the cabin door opens at CUN, the air smells of salt and sunscreen, and you have spent exactly zero minutes in a terminal.

Cancún is really two places. The Hotel Zone is a slim strip of sand shaped like the number 7, with the Caribbean on one side and the calm Nichupté Lagoon on the other. Downtown is where locals eat cochinita pibil at Mercado 28 and families stroll the Parque de las Palapas at night. Drive south and the Riviera Maya opens up: Puerto Morelos, Playa del Carmen, Tulum, and hundreds of cenotes, the freshwater sinkholes the ancient Maya saw as doorways to the underworld.

Door to door

Your day, from Mexico City to the beach

  1. 7:30 am

    Leave your home or office

    Your driver picks you up at home and heads west on the México-Toluca highway. Plan on about an hour, depending on traffic. Passports can stay in the drawer, since an official photo ID is all you need on this domestic flight.

  2. 8:35 am

    Boarding at Toluca FBO

    The car pulls up at the private terminal (FBO) at Toluca International. A quick ID check, your bags go aboard, and you are at the aircraft steps in under 10 minutes from the curb. Breakfast and coffee are already on board.

  3. 8:45 am

    Wheels up, heading east

    Breakfast is served as you climb out of the Valley of Toluca. Somewhere past Veracruz the Gulf of Mexico fills the window, and for much of the flight you have nothing below you but blue water and scattered clouds.

  4. 12:00 pm local

    Landing at CUN

    You touch down at Cancún International after about 2 hours 15 minutes. Quintana Roo runs one hour ahead of Mexico City, so local time reads noon. Your car is waiting on the private aviation side of the airport.

  5. 12:45 pm

    Lunch with sand underfoot

    Twenty minutes later you are in the Hotel Zone, shoes off, ordering ceviche and a cold michelada facing the reef. For Playa del Carmen or Tulum, the drive south on Highway 307 starts right outside the airport.

At your destination

What to do in Cancún after you land

Best time to fly to Cancún

December through April is the dry season, with sunny days around 28 degrees Celsius and lower humidity. It is also high season: Christmas, New Year, US spring break in March and Semana Santa fill the hotels, so confirm your aircraft as early as you can for those weeks. May is hot and quieter. The Atlantic hurricane season officially runs from June 1 to November 30, with most storms between August and October, and sargassum seaweed can wash onto some beaches in spring and summer. Late November is a sweet spot, with good weather, calm seas and lower hotel rates before the holidays.

Arriving at Cancún International (CUN)

Your aircraft lands at Cancún International Airport (CUN), about 1,280 km from Toluca and the busiest airport in southeastern Mexico. Private flights are handled on the general aviation side, away from the commercial terminals, so you skip the arrivals hall and the crowd of sales desks at the exit. The Hotel Zone is about 20 minutes away by car, downtown about 25, Puerto Morelos around 25, Playa del Carmen about 45 and Tulum just under two hours. This is a domestic flight, so there is no customs or immigration on arrival.

Documents for a private flight to Cancún

Your flight to Cancún is domestic, so Mexican nationals only need a valid official photo ID, such as an INE card or a passport. Foreign visitors should carry the passport they used to enter Mexico. Send the full name of every passenger on WhatsApp when you confirm, and the aircraft can be ready about 2 hours later. Customs and immigration do not apply on this route.

How long is a private jet flight from Mexico City to Cancún?

About 2 hours 15 minutes nonstop from Toluca (AIT) to Cancún International (CUN). Add roughly an hour by car from Polanco, Santa Fe, Lomas or Interlomas to Toluca and under 10 minutes from the curb to the aircraft steps. Cancún runs one hour ahead of Mexico City, so set your watch forward on landing.

Do you go through TSA when flying private?

TSA is the US screening agency, and it plays no part in a domestic flight from Toluca to Cancún. At the Toluca private terminal there is a short ID check and baggage screening, then you walk to the aircraft. From car to aircraft steps takes under 10 minutes, with no lines and no gate.

Do you tip a private jet pilot?

It is never expected. Your JETCAB quote already covers the crew, along with fuel, catering and permits, so there is nothing extra to pay on the day. Some passengers leave a gratuity for the crew after a long weekend or a special occasion, and that is entirely your choice.
