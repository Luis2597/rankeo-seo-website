The trip

The fastest way out of a city of 22 million

Mexico City rewards the people who live in it with museums, restaurants and parks few capitals can match, and punishes them with traffic. A Friday afternoon on Periférico can eat two hours before you even reach a terminal. That is why so many families in Polanco, Lomas and Santa Fe stopped flying commercial for their weekends and work trips. They leave from Toluca, a short drive or helicopter hop to the west, where JETCAB has operated since 1999 and where a private jet Mexico City travelers book is already fueled and waiting.

The difference shows at both ends of the trip. You arrive at a private terminal 15 minutes before departure, walk from the car to the aircraft steps in under 10 minutes, and land in Cancún, Monterrey or Los Cabos without a gate change or a baggage carousel. Coming home works the same way. You touch down in Toluca on Sunday evening, skip the arrivals hall entirely, and can be back on your terrace in Polanco while the airline crowd is still waiting for its bags.

Door to door

Polanco to the Caribbean before lunch

    Leave home in Polanco

    Your driver collects you on a quiet street off Masaryk and heads west through Lomas and Santa Fe toward Toluca. Allow 45 to 60 minutes by car, or about 25 minutes if you take the helicopter transfer instead.

  2. 8:30 am

    Board at Toluca FBO

    At the private terminal, bags go straight to the aircraft and IDs are checked inside. You walk from the car to the aircraft steps in under 10 minutes, with no counters, no gate and no boarding groups.

  3. 8:45 am

    Over the volcanoes

    The aircraft turns east and climbs past the snow on Popocatépetl and Iztaccíhuatl on a clear day. Breakfast is served on board, and the cabin is yours, so the kids can spread out and the calls stay private.

  4. 11:00 am

    Landing in Cancún

    About 2 hours 15 minutes after takeoff, you land at Cancún International Airport (CUN). The heat and the salt in the air hit you at the door, and the car you arranged is waiting at the private terminal.

  5. 11:45 am

    Toes in the sand

    About 20 minutes up the road you reach your hotel in the Hotel Zone, with the Caribbean turning turquoise in the midday sun. You left Polanco roughly four hours ago, and lunch is still ahead of you.

Best time to fly private from Mexico City

Flights leave Mexico City year-round. The dry season, from November to April, brings clear mornings and the best views of the volcanoes on the climb out of Toluca. The rainy season, roughly June to September, brings short afternoon storms, so morning departures tend to run smoother. Demand peaks during Semana Santa, the summer school break, the long weekend around Día de Muertos and the weeks between Christmas and New Year, when everyone in the capital seems to head for Cancún, Los Cabos or Puerto Vallarta at once. Book those dates at least a week ahead. For the rest of the year, domestic aircraft are ready about 2 hours after you confirm.

Which airport private jets use in Mexico City

JETCAB flights for Mexico City depart from Toluca International Airport (IATA TLC, ICAO MMTO), 65 km west of the financial district. From Polanco, Lomas or Reforma, the airport is 45 to 60 minutes by car outside rush hour, or about 25 minutes by helicopter. Private passengers use an FBO separate from the commercial terminal, so you can arrive 15 minutes before departure and walk from the car to the aircraft in under 10 minutes. On the way home, you land at the same FBO, skip the arrivals hall, and your car is waiting outside. International returns clear Mexican immigration and customs at the airport on arrival.

Documents for private flights from Mexico City

Domestic flights need an official photo ID for every passenger: an INE card or passport for Mexican citizens, a passport for foreign visitors. Flights to the United States need a valid passport and, for Mexican citizens, a US visa, plus about 24 hours so JETCAB can file the eAPIS manifest with US Customs. Flights to Cuba, Spain or the rest of Europe need passports and overflight and landing permits, which JETCAB arranges in 48 to 72 hours.

How long does it take to get from Mexico City to Toluca Airport?

Can you fly a private plane from the US to Mexico City?

Yes. JETCAB flies from Houston, Miami, Los Angeles, New York and other US cities into Toluca, the main private aviation airport for a private jet Mexico City trip. Mexican immigration and customs clear on arrival at the airport, and you can be in Santa Fe or Polanco within the hour.

Can I fly to another Mexican city and back the same day?

Yes, and many clients do. Guadalajara is 50 minutes from Toluca and Monterrey about 1 hour 15 minutes, so you can fly out in the morning, hold a full day of meetings and be back in Mexico City for dinner. The aircraft can wait for you at the destination airport.
