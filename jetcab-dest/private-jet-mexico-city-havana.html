The trip

Havana, in its own time

Havana rewards slow travel. In Old Havana, restored plazas sit beside weathered baroque facades, and the sound of a son band drifts out of a courtyard at noon. Fifties Chevrolets in candy colors idle along the Paseo del Prado. In the evening the whole city seems to walk the Malecón, the seawall that curves for about eight kilometers along the Straits of Florida, while waves throw spray over the stone. It feels like nowhere else in the Caribbean.

A private jet to Cuba turns a complicated trip into a short one. Commercial service from Mexico City runs on airline schedules; with JETCAB you choose the hour, land at José Martí about two and a half hours after leaving Toluca, and head into the city with your group. JETCAB arranges the Cuban landing permit, overflight authorizations and handling in Havana, so the biggest decision left to you is which paladar to book for dinner.

Door to door

Your day from Mexico City to Havana

  1. 6:50 am

    Leave your home or office

    Your driver collects you early for the 45 to 60 minute drive west to Toluca. To save the highway time, the JETCAB helicopter transfer from Polanco, Lomas, Santa Fe or Reforma covers it in about 25 minutes.

  2. 8:00 am

    Boarding at Toluca FBO

    At the private terminal your car stops close to the aircraft, and you board in under 10 minutes. Keep your passport and Cuban entry documents at hand for the Mexican exit check, then settle in while bags are loaded.

  3. 8:15 am

    East over the Gulf

    Wheels up toward the Yucatán and across the Gulf of Mexico. Breakfast is served, the cabin is quiet, and about 2 hours 30 minutes later the green coast of western Cuba appears below the wing.

  4. 11:45 am

    Landing at José Martí

    You land at Havana's José Martí International around 11:45 am local time; Havana is one hour ahead of Mexico City in winter and two in summer. The local handling agent guides your group through immigration and customs.

  5. 12:30 pm

    Lunch in Old Havana

    Old Havana is about 30 minutes away by road. Drop the bags at your hotel or casa particular, then sit down for lunch near Plaza Vieja, where the restored colonial buildings glow in the early afternoon light.

At your destination

What to do in Havana after your private jet to Cuba lands

When to fly to Cuba

The dry season from November to April is the most comfortable time to visit, with warm days, lower humidity and calm seas. December through March is peak season, so plan accommodation early. The Havana International Jazz Festival is usually held around the turn of the year, and late winter brings the Habanos Festival for cigar lovers. June through November is hurricane season, with the highest risk from August to October, so keep dates flexible then. International clearances take 48 to 72 hours, so request your quote at least three days before you want to fly.

Arriving at José Martí International (HAV)

José Martí International Airport sits about 15 kilometers south of central Havana. Private flights are received by a local handling agent who guides your group through Cuban immigration and customs, and JETCAB coordinates that handling together with the landing permit before you leave Toluca. Old Havana is about 30 minutes away by road, Vedado and the Malecón around 25 minutes, and Varadero roughly two hours east. Plan your payments before you travel, since cards issued by US banks do not work in Cuba.

Documents and travel rules for Cuba

Every passenger needs a passport valid for the length of the stay, a Cuban tourist card or visa, and travel medical insurance. Cuba also asks travelers to complete the D'Viajeros online form before arrival. US citizens and residents remain subject to US government rules that restrict travel to Cuba, so check current US Treasury and State Department guidance before you book. JETCAB handles the aircraft permits, which take 48 to 72 hours.

Questions

Havana private jet FAQ

How long is a private jet flight from Mexico City to Cuba?

About 2 hours 30 minutes from Toluca (AIT) to José Martí International in Havana (HAV), a distance of roughly 1,900 km. Havana is one hour ahead of Mexico City in winter, so a jet leaving Toluca at 8:15 am lands at about 11:45 am local time, in time for lunch.

How much does a private jet to Cuba cost?

A private jet to Cuba from Mexico City starts at $1,800 USD one way for the full aircraft, with crew, fuel, catering and permits included, along with the Cuban landing permit. Larger cabins and return legs are quoted on WhatsApp within 30 minutes.

Can US citizens fly private to Cuba from Mexico?

US rules on travel to Cuba apply to US citizens and residents no matter which country they depart from, and those rules restrict tourism. Before booking, check the current guidance from the US Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control and the State Department. JETCAB arranges the aircraft permits, while personal eligibility is each traveler's responsibility.

Can planes fly over Cuba?

Yes. Cuban airspace is crossed every day by international flights, which file overflight authorizations and pay the related fees. For a flight landing in Havana, JETCAB requests both the overflight and landing permits as part of the booking, which is why international departures need 48 to 72 hours.

Do you tip a private jet pilot?

Tipping is not required, and no gratuity is ever added to a JETCAB quote because the price already covers the crew. If a pilot or flight attendant went out of their way for your group, a tip is a personal gesture left entirely to your judgment.

What is the cheapest way to fly privately to Cuba?

Start with the Learjet 35, which seats up to 7 and begins at $1,800 USD one way to Havana for the whole aircraft. With every seat filled, the cost per person drops considerably. Flexible dates help too, since an empty leg heading the right way can occasionally match your plans.
