The trip

Friday night in the desert

Las Vegas does not ease you in. You step off the aircraft into dry desert air, and minutes later the Strip is lit up outside the car window: the Bellagio fountains, the glowing Sphere next to the Venetian, a line of towers that seems to run straight into the mountains. A private jet to Las Vegas makes the trip feel like part of the celebration, with your group together in one cabin, the music you chose and nobody to share the armrest with.

Most people come for a reason. A big fight on Mexican Independence Day weekend or Cinco de Mayo. A birthday, a bachelor party, a trade show at the convention center. The Formula 1 race that runs down the Strip in November. With JETCAB, you can leave Toluca on Friday afternoon, be at dinner on the Strip that night and fly home on Sunday, in time to start the week in Mexico City as if you had never left.

Door to door

A Friday departure, from Polanco to the Strip

  1. 1:00 pm

    Leave home in Polanco

    Your driver heads west to Toluca International Airport, 45 to 60 minutes by car from Polanco. Friday afternoon traffic can be heavy, so a helicopter transfer of about 25 minutes is worth considering for this trip.

  2. 2:00 pm

    Board at Toluca FBO

    Passports are checked inside the private terminal and the bags go straight to the aircraft. Your group walks from the cars to the aircraft steps in under 10 minutes, with the eAPIS manifest filed the day before.

  3. 2:15 pm

    North over the desert

    The flight heads north across the Sierra Madre and the deserts of Sonora and Arizona. Drinks and catering are served on board, and with the cabin to yourselves, the weekend starts the moment the door closes.

  4. 6:05 pm CDMX

    Landing in Las Vegas

    About 3 hours 50 minutes after takeoff, you land at Harry Reid or Henderson Executive. Las Vegas runs one or two hours behind Mexico City, depending on the season, so the evening has barely started.

  5. First hour

    Check in, then dinner

    Your cars take you to the Strip, where you check in, change and head down to dinner. By the time the Bellagio fountains start their evening shows, your group is seated and the weekend is under way.

At your destination

What to do in Las Vegas after your private jet lands

Best time to fly private to Las Vegas

Spring, from March to May, and fall, from late September to November, bring the best weather, with warm days and cool desert nights. Summer is very hot, often above 105 degrees Fahrenheit, which pushes life indoors and into the pools. Winter days are mild and nights can be cold. Some weekends book out months ahead: CES in early January, the big boxing cards on Cinco de Mayo and Mexican Independence Day weekends, the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix in November, the National Finals Rodeo in December and New Year's Eve on the Strip. For those dates, reserve your aircraft and hotel as early as you can.

Arriving in Las Vegas by private jet: LAS or HND

JETCAB flies to Harry Reid International Airport (LAS), close to the south end of the Strip, or to Henderson Executive Airport (HND), a smaller and quieter airport south of the city. At both, private aircraft use FBOs separate from the airline terminals, so you walk from the aircraft to your car without crowds or baggage carousels. The drive to the Strip is short from either one. US Customs and Border Protection clears you on arrival, using the eAPIS manifest JETCAB files about 24 hours before departure.

Documents for a private flight to Las Vegas

Every passenger needs a valid passport, and Mexican citizens also need a valid US visa. Travelers of other nationalities need the entry document their passport requires for the United States. JETCAB files the eAPIS manifest with US Customs and Border Protection, so we need names, dates of birth and passport details for the whole group about 24 hours before departure. Last-minute guests should be confirmed before then.

Las Vegas private jet FAQ

How long is a private jet flight from Mexico City to Las Vegas?

About 3 hours 50 minutes from Toluca International Airport (TLC) to Harry Reid International (LAS) or Henderson Executive (HND), a distance of roughly 2,800 km. Add 45 to 60 minutes by car from Polanco to Toluca, or about 25 minutes by helicopter.

How much does a private jet to Las Vegas cost?

From Mexico City, a private jet to Las Vegas starts at $15,000 USD one way per aircraft, with crew, fuel, catering and permits included. Other aircraft are quoted in 30 minutes on WhatsApp. Because the price covers the whole aircraft, the cost per person drops as your group grows.

How much is a private jet for 4 hours?

Our closest example is Las Vegas, about 3 hours 50 minutes from Toluca, which starts at $15,000 USD one way per aircraft. Prices for other routes of similar length depend on the aircraft and the date, and JETCAB confirms an exact quote within 30 minutes.

Can JETCAB arrange a weekend trip to Las Vegas?

Yes. A Friday departure with a Sunday return is the classic plan. US flights need about 24 hours of notice for the eAPIS manifest, but for fight weekends, Formula 1 and New Year's Eve, book as early as possible so the aircraft and your hotel are both secured.

What documents do I need to fly private to Las Vegas?

A valid passport for every passenger and, for Mexican citizens, a valid US visa. JETCAB needs passenger details about 24 hours before departure to file the eAPIS manifest with US Customs. Officers check your documents when you land, at the private terminal.

Do you tip a private jet pilot?

Tipping is not expected, because crew costs are already included in the charter price. If the crew made your group's trip especially smooth, a thank-you note or a gratuity handed to the captain is a kind gesture. It is always optional and never assumed.
