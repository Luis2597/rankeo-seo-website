The trip

The city on the line

Tijuana has outgrown every label it was given. The old souvenir strip of Avenida Revolución now shares the street with craft breweries and cocktail bars, and chefs from Zona Río to Playas de Tijuana cook a style the rest of Mexico calls Baja Med: Pacific seafood, olive oil and northern Mexican fire. It is also home to the busiest land crossing in the Western Hemisphere, a city where doctors, factories and families work on both sides of the line every day.

Getting there from central Mexico is the hard part. Commercial schedules work for some trips, while a private jet to Tijuana lets you set the hour, bring the whole team and land at TIJ in about three hours. Patients arrive rested for their appointment. Executives visit a plant in Otay Mesa and fly home the same evening. Couples land in the morning and are pouring a glass of Baja Nebbiolo in Valle de Guadalupe by early afternoon.

Door to door

Your day from Polanco to Tijuana

  1. 6:50 am

    Leave Polanco

    Your driver collects you before the morning traffic builds. Toluca is 45 to 60 minutes west by road, or about 25 minutes by JETCAB helicopter from Polanco, Lomas, Santa Fe or Reforma if you would rather skip the highway.

  2. 8:00 am

    Boarding at Toluca FBO

    Your car stops at the private terminal and you reach the aircraft steps in under 10 minutes. On a domestic flight your official ID is all you show. Bags go straight into the hold and breakfast is already loaded on board.

  3. 8:15 am

    Across western Mexico

    Wheels up. The route crosses western Mexico toward the Gulf of California and the Sonoran desert, with the ridges of the Sierra Madre Occidental below the wing on a clear day. Work, sleep or eat at your own pace.

  4. 9:25 am

    Landing at TIJ

    You land at Tijuana International around 9:25 am Pacific time in winter, after about 3 hours 10 minutes. The airport sits right on the border in Otay Mesa. With no customs on a domestic arrival, your car is ready within minutes.

  5. 10:00 am

    Breakfast in Zona Río

    Zona Río, the business and dining district along the Tijuana River, is about 15 minutes away. Start with coffee and a Baja breakfast, then head to your appointment, your plant visit or the coast road south to Rosarito.

At your destination

What to do in Tijuana after you land

When to fly a private jet to Tijuana

Tijuana has a dry, mild climate shaped by the Pacific. Summer days sit in the high 70s and low 80s°F, winters are cool with most of the year's rain falling between December and March, and spring mornings often start under coastal fog. July and August are harvest season in Valle de Guadalupe, when the Fiestas de la Vendimia bring tastings and long dinners among the vines, so reserve lodging early. For medical or business trips, any month works. Domestic flights can be ready about 2 hours after you confirm, though a day's notice gives you the widest choice of aircraft.

Arriving at Tijuana International (TIJ)

Tijuana International, officially General Abelardo L. Rodríguez International Airport, sits in Otay Mesa with its runway running alongside the US border fence. Private aircraft use the general aviation side, away from the commercial terminal. Because your flight from Toluca is domestic, there is no customs or immigration on arrival. Zona Río is about 15 minutes away, Playas de Tijuana about 25, Rosarito 30 to 40 and Valle de Guadalupe roughly an hour and a half to two hours. The Cross Border Xpress bridge to the San Diego side connects to the commercial terminal.

Documents for a private flight to Tijuana

Tijuana is a domestic flight, so Mexican citizens only need an official photo ID such as the INE or a passport. Foreign passengers carry their passport and the immigration record they entered Mexico with. Send the passenger names when you book so the manifest is ready. If you plan to cross into San Diego during the trip, bring your passport and a valid US visa, since the land border formalities still apply.

Questions

Tijuana private jet FAQ

How long is a private jet flight from Mexico City to Tijuana?

About 3 hours 10 minutes nonstop from Toluca (AIT) to Tijuana International (TIJ), a distance of roughly 2,600 km. Tijuana runs on Pacific time, one to two hours behind Mexico City, so a jet leaving Toluca at 8:15 am lands at about 9:25 am local time in winter.

How much does a private jet to Tijuana cost?

A private jet to Tijuana from Mexico City starts at $12,000 USD one way for the full aircraft, with crew, fuel, catering and permits included. If you need a larger cabin or a same-day return, JETCAB confirms the exact price on WhatsApp within 30 minutes.

Do I need a passport to fly private to Tijuana?

Mexican citizens do not. Toluca to Tijuana is a domestic route, so an official photo ID such as the INE is enough. Foreign visitors carry their passport and Mexican entry record. Anyone planning to continue to San Diego by land needs a passport and a valid US visa.

Can I reach San Diego from Tijuana after landing?

Yes. The San Ysidro and Otay Mesa crossings are a short drive from TIJ, though car lines can be long at peak hours. If San Diego is your real destination, a direct private jet to San Diego takes about 3 hours 20 minutes from $9,000 USD and skips the land border entirely.

How much is a private jet to Cabo?

From Mexico City, a private jet to Los Cabos (SJD) takes about 2 hours 30 minutes and starts at $3,800 USD one way on a Learjet 35 for up to 7 passengers. You can also pair both ends of Baja in one trip, with business in Tijuana and a few days at the tip of the peninsula.

Do you tip a private jet pilot?

It is not required, and no tip is ever added to a JETCAB quote, since the price already covers the crew. If someone on board went out of their way for you or your family, a gratuity is a personal gesture left entirely to your judgment.
