The trip

Where the desert meets the sea

Los Cabos sits at the very bottom of the Baja California peninsula, 1,700 km from Toluca, where the Sea of Cortez finally opens into the Pacific Ocean. The flight northwest crosses the Sierra Madre and then a long stretch of open gulf. On approach the landscape turns to cardón cactus, granite and pale beaches that run for miles, and the first thing you notice when the door opens is the dry desert heat with a faint edge of salt.

Two towns share this coast. San José del Cabo is the quieter one, with a whitewashed mission church, a leafy plaza and an art district that holds a Thursday night art walk in season. Cabo San Lucas, about 30 km west along the Tourist Corridor, is the marina, the sportfishing fleet and the famous rock arch at Land's End. Between them, cliffside resorts and golf courses look out over a sea that Jacques Cousteau called the aquarium of the world.

Door to door

From Polanco to the Tourist Corridor

  1. 1:00 pm

    Leave Polanco

    Close the laptop after an early lunch and head west toward Toluca on the México-Toluca highway. Count on about an hour in the car. An official photo ID is all you need for this domestic flight, so the paperwork is already done.

  2. 2:05 pm

    Boarding at Toluca FBO

    The car pulls up at the private terminal (FBO) at Toluca International. A quick ID check, your bags go aboard, and you are at the aircraft steps in under 10 minutes from the curb. There is no gate to find.

  3. 2:15 pm

    Northwest over the gulf

    A late lunch or a cold drink as you climb out of the valley. The last part of the flight crosses the Gulf of California, and on a clear day the Baja peninsula appears ahead as a long brown line on the horizon.

  4. 3:45 pm local

    Landing at SJD

    Touchdown at Los Cabos International after about 2 hours 30 minutes. Baja California Sur runs one hour behind Mexico City, so you gain an hour of daylight. Your car is waiting on the private aviation side of the airport.

  5. 4:30 pm

    Sunset on the Corridor

    Fifteen minutes puts you in San José del Cabo, about forty-five in Cabo San Lucas. Check in, take a swim, and be on a terrace facing the water with a cold drink when the sun goes down over the Pacific.

At your destination

What to do in Cabo after your private jet lands

Best time to fly to Los Cabos

Los Cabos is sunny and dry most of the year, so a private jet to Cabo works in any month. December through April is whale season, when humpback whales arrive from colder northern waters to breed and calve, and it is also the busiest stretch for private flights. Christmas, New Year and Semana Santa book up first, so send those dates early. May and June are hot, dry and calm. July through October brings heat, humidity and the chance of Pacific storms, and October is also the month of the big billfish tournaments out of Cabo San Lucas. November is warm and clear, a favorite with golfers.

Arriving at Los Cabos International (SJD)

Your jet lands at Los Cabos International (SJD), just north of San José del Cabo and about 1,700 km from Toluca. Private aircraft are handled through the general aviation side of the airport, apart from the commercial terminals and their crowds, and your car can meet you there. Downtown San José is about 15 minutes away, the hotels of the Tourist Corridor 20 to 35 minutes, Cabo San Lucas around 45 and Todos Santos about an hour and a half. This is a domestic flight, so there is no customs stop.

Documents for a private flight to Los Cabos

Your flight to Los Cabos is domestic, so Mexican nationals only need a valid official photo ID, such as an INE card or a passport. Foreign visitors should carry the passport they used to enter Mexico. Send the full name of every passenger on WhatsApp when you confirm, and the aircraft can be ready about 2 hours later. Customs and immigration do not apply on this route.

Questions

Los Cabos private jet FAQ

How much is a private jet to Cabo?

A private jet to Cabo from Toluca starts at $3,800 USD one way on a Learjet 35 for up to 7 passengers, per aircraft, with crew, fuel, catering and permits included. The Challenger 605 for up to 12 and the Gulfstream GV for up to 16 are quoted in 30 minutes for your dates.

How long is a private jet flight from Mexico City to Los Cabos?

About 2 hours 30 minutes nonstop from Toluca (AIT) to Los Cabos International (SJD), 365 days a year. Baja California Sur is one hour behind Mexico City, so a 2:15 pm departure lands at about 3:45 pm local time, early enough for a swim before dinner.

What is an empty leg?

An empty leg is a flight an aircraft has to make without passengers, usually to reposition after dropping off another client. Operators sometimes offer those legs below the normal charter price. The date and direction are fixed by the other trip, so they suit flexible plans. Ask JETCAB on WhatsApp whether one lines up with your dates.

When is whale season in Los Cabos?

Roughly December through April, when humpback whales gather off the tip of the peninsula to breed and calve. It is the peak season for private flights to Los Cabos, together with the Christmas holidays and Semana Santa, so confirming your aircraft early is the safest move.

Do you tip a private jet pilot?

It is never expected. Your JETCAB quote already covers the crew, along with fuel, catering and permits, so there is nothing extra to pay on the day. Some passengers leave a gratuity for the crew after a long weekend or a special occasion, and that is entirely your choice.

Is Los Cabos accessible year-round by private jet?

Yes. JETCAB flies the Toluca to Los Cabos route all year. Summer and early fall are hot and humid with the chance of Pacific storms, and the crew will advise you if weather affects a departure. Winter and spring bring the most stable flying weather and the busiest demand.
