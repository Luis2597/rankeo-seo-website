The trip

Four capitals, one overnight crossing

Europe is a short list of very specific desires. An early table at a bistro in the Marais. The fashion showrooms of Milan's Quadrilatero della Moda. A Saturday on the King's Road in London, or a walk along Lake Geneva before a meeting with a private bank. Each sits a single night's flight from Mexico City, and each looks different when you arrive rested, on your own schedule, with the whole afternoon still ahead of you.

A private jet to Europe makes the crossing simple. JETCAB's long-range Gulfstream GV and Global Express fly from Toluca without a fuel stop to the continent's major business aviation airports, from Le Bourget and Malpensa to Geneva, Zurich, Frankfurt, Amsterdam and Lisbon. Overflight permits, arrival handling and customs coordination are arranged before you board. You choose the city, the date and the hour, and the crew plans the night around your sleep.

Door to door

Your overnight journey from Mexico City to Paris

  1. 6:30 pm

    Leave your home or office

    Your driver collects you in the early evening for the 45 to 60 minute drive west to Toluca. With less time to spare, the JETCAB helicopter transfer from Polanco, Lomas, Santa Fe or Reforma takes about 25 minutes.

  2. 7:45 pm

    Boarding at Toluca FBO

    Car to aircraft steps in under 10 minutes. Mexican exit immigration is handled at the private terminal, your luggage goes into the hold, and the crew settles you into the cabin for the night ahead.

  3. 8:00 pm

    Dinner at altitude

    Wheels up toward the northeast. Dinner is served as the jet crosses the Gulf of Mexico, then the cabin lights dim and the seats recline for the night. The crossing to Paris takes about 11 hours.

  4. 2:00 pm

    Landing at Le Bourget

    You touch down at Paris-Le Bourget around 2:00 pm local time in winter; Paris is seven hours ahead of Mexico City in winter and eight in summer. Passport control is handled at the business aviation terminal.

  5. 2:45 pm

    Afternoon on the Seine

    Central Paris is about 30 to 40 minutes away by car. Check in, walk down to the Seine and take a late lunch on a café terrace, letting the city reset your body clock before dinner.

At your destination

Where to fly in Europe from Mexico City

When to fly a private jet to Europe

May, June and September are the sweet spot for most of Europe, with long days, mild weather and full cultural calendars. July and August bring heat in the south and the peak villa season on the Mediterranean, while many Parisians leave the city. Milan hosts its fashion weeks in February and September and Design Week in April. December brings Christmas markets in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, and the Alps stay open for skiing into spring. Transatlantic flights need 48 to 72 hours for overflight permits, and peak dates call for booking several weeks ahead.

Private arrivals across Europe

Every major European city has an airport or terminal set up for private aircraft. Paris arrivals use Le Bourget (LBG), Milan uses Malpensa (MXP), and Geneva (GVA) and Zurich (ZRH) have dedicated business aviation terminals. Frankfurt (FRA), Amsterdam (AMS), Lisbon (LIS) and London are also served, with the arrival airport confirmed in your quote. In each case JETCAB arranges the handling agent, passport control and customs in advance, so your group clears formalities apart from the main halls and meets its car within minutes of landing.

Documents for a private jet to Europe

Mexican and US citizens currently visit the Schengen area without a visa for up to 90 days in any 180-day period, with a passport valid at least three months beyond departure. The EU's Entry/Exit System now registers non-EU travelers biometrically, and the ETIAS authorization is due to start, so check its status before you fly. The United Kingdom requires an Electronic Travel Authorisation for Mexican and US visitors. JETCAB arranges overflight and landing permits, which take 48 to 72 hours.

Questions

Europe private jet FAQ

How much does it cost to fly a private jet to Europe?

A private jet to Europe from Mexico City starts at $100,000 USD one way for the whole aircraft, on a Gulfstream GV or Global Express for up to 16 passengers. Crew, fuel, catering, permits and handling are included. Your exact price, including any return leg, is confirmed in 30 minutes on WhatsApp.

How long is a private jet flight from Mexico to Europe?

From Toluca (AIT), London takes about 10.5 hours and Paris about 11 hours nonstop, with Madrid at roughly 11 hours and Eastern Europe at 12 hours or more. An evening departure means sleeping through most of the crossing and landing in the early afternoon, local time.

Can a private jet fly from Mexico to Europe without stopping?

Yes, with the right aircraft. Long-range jets such as the Gulfstream GV and Global Express can cross the Atlantic from Toluca without a technical stop. Smaller jets like the Learjet 35 lack that range, which is why every European route is flown on a long-range cabin.

Which European cities can I fly to from Mexico City?

JETCAB flies to the major European capitals and business centers, including London, Paris Le Bourget, Milan Malpensa, Geneva, Zurich, Frankfurt, Amsterdam, Lisbon, Madrid and Barcelona. You can also plan a multi-city trip, such as a few days of meetings in Paris followed by a long weekend on Lake Como.

What is the cheapest way to fly a private jet to Europe?

Fill the cabin and plan ahead. With up to 16 seats, a long-range jet shared by a large family or a company delegation brings the cost per person well below the headline price. Booking early for peak dates and asking about empty legs on the return can also help.

Do you tip a private jet pilot?

Tipping is not required, and no gratuity is ever added to a JETCAB quote because the price already covers the crew. After a long overnight crossing, if the crew went out of their way for your group, a tip is a personal gesture left entirely to you.
