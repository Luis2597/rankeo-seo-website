The trip

Where Mexico City's private flights begin

Most people who fly private out of Mexico City never set foot in the capital's main airport. They head west on the highway toward Toluca, climbing through pine forest into the high valley of the State of Mexico, where Toluca International Airport sits at more than 2,500 meters above sea level. JETCAB has operated from here since 1999. The airspace is calmer than over the city, and the private terminal is small enough that the staff remember you. On a clear morning, the Nevado de Toluca fills the windshield before you reach the gate.

The drive from Polanco or Santa Fe takes 45 to 60 minutes outside rush hour, and a helicopter transfer cuts it to about 25. Once you arrive, the process is short. Your bags go straight to the aircraft, your ID is checked inside the FBO, and you walk from the car to the aircraft steps in under 10 minutes. For a family heading to the beach or a team flying to a plant visit in Monterrey, a private jet from Toluca gives back the hours that disappear at a commercial terminal.

At your destination

Around Toluca: what to see before or after your flight

Best time to fly private from Toluca

Toluca flies year-round, but the valley has its own weather. From November to February, mornings can start near freezing and fog sometimes sits over the runway at dawn, so mid-morning departures are the safer choice. The rainy season runs roughly from June to September, with afternoon storms that usually clear by evening. The busiest weeks are Semana Santa, the summer school break, the long weekend around Día de Muertos and the December holidays, when beach routes to Cancún and Los Cabos fill quickly. For those dates, book a week or more ahead. The rest of the year, domestic aircraft are ready about 2 hours after you confirm.

Toluca International Airport (TLC) for private flights

Licenciado Adolfo López Mateos International Airport in Toluca (IATA TLC, ICAO MMTO) is JETCAB's home base and the main private aviation airport for Mexico City. It sits 65 km west of the capital's financial district, 45 to 60 minutes by car from Polanco or Reforma, or about 25 minutes by helicopter. Private flights use an FBO apart from the commercial terminal, so there are no check-in counters and no long security lines. Arriving 15 minutes before departure is enough for a domestic flight. On international departures, exit formalities are completed at the airport before you board, with JETCAB coordinating the paperwork in advance.

Documents you need to fly from Toluca

For flights inside Mexico, Mexican citizens need an official photo ID such as an INE card or a passport, and foreign visitors need their passport. For the United States, every traveler needs a valid passport, and Mexican citizens also need a US visa. JETCAB files the eAPIS manifest with US Customs, which is why US flights need about 24 hours of notice. Other international destinations need passports plus overflight and landing permits, which take 48 to 72 hours.

Questions

Toluca private jet FAQ

Why do private jets from Mexico City leave from Toluca?

Toluca International Airport has dedicated FBO facilities and none of the commercial congestion of the capital's main airport, so boarding is faster and fully private. It has also been JETCAB's operating base since 1999, which means the aircraft, crews and ground staff are already there when you book a private jet from Toluca.

How far is Toluca Airport from Mexico City?

Toluca International Airport (TLC) is 65 km west of Mexico City's financial district. By car, the drive from Polanco or Reforma takes 45 to 60 minutes outside rush hour. A helicopter transfer covers the same distance in about 25 minutes and sets you down at the airport.

How much does it cost to fly privately in Mexico?

A private jet from Toluca on a Learjet 35, for up to 7 passengers, starts at $1,800 USD one way to Guadalajara, $2,200 to Monterrey, $3,200 to Cancún and $3,800 to Los Cabos. Prices are per aircraft, with crew, fuel, catering and permits included. Any other route is quoted in 30 minutes.

How much does a 2-hour private jet flight cost?

Cancún is the closest example from Toluca, at about 2 hours 15 minutes. It starts at $3,200 USD one way on a Learjet 35, $6,500 on a Challenger 605 and $12,000 on a Gulfstream GV. Because you pay for the whole aircraft, a full Learjet works out to under $500 per seat.

How early do I need to arrive at Toluca for a private flight?

Fifteen minutes before departure is enough for a domestic flight. There is no check-in counter or security line at the FBO, and the walk from your car to the aircraft steps takes under 10 minutes. For international flights, allow some extra time for passport checks before boarding.

Do you go through TSA when flying private?

TSA only operates inside the United States, so it does not apply when you leave Toluca. At the FBO, your ID and bags are checked in a few minutes. When you land in the US, CBP officers clear you at the private terminal of your first US airport, using the eAPIS manifest JETCAB files ahead of time.
