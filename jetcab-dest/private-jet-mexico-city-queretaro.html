The trip

Colonial stone and jet engines

Querétaro is two cities at once. The historic center is a UNESCO World Heritage Site of pink and ochre stone, baroque churches and quiet plazas, where the conspiracy that set off Mexico's War of Independence was planned in 1810. Its aqueduct, finished in 1735, still strides across the eastern side of town on 74 arches, the tallest close to 30 meters high, and at night the whole center glows under warm lights.

Around that old core, Querétaro has become one of Mexico's fastest-growing industrial and aerospace hubs, with aircraft parts, automotive suppliers and data centers spread across its industrial parks. The countryside is just as rewarding. East of the city, the wine and cheese route runs through Tequisquiapan and Ezequiel Montes, and the giant monolith of Peña de Bernal rises over the fields. San Miguel de Allende is about an hour away by road.

    The main industrial parks are 15 to 30 minutes from the airport and the historic center about 30. Run your meetings, have lunch in a courtyard restaurant near the Plaza de Armas, and fly home in the afternoon.

At your destination

What to see in Querétaro after you land

Best time to fly to Querétaro

Querétaro sits on the high plateau at about 1,800 meters, so days are warm and nights are cool for most of the year. October to May is the dry season, with clear skies that suit both business trips and weekends in wine country. April and May are the warmest months. From June to September afternoon showers are common, and this is also harvest time, when the vineyards around Tequisquiapan and Ezequiel Montes hold their grape-harvest festivals. Business travel runs all year, and a private jet to Querétaro is easy to arrange on short notice, with domestic flights ready about 2 hours after confirmation.

Arriving at Querétaro Intercontinental (QRO)

Querétaro Intercontinental Airport (QRO) is about 220 km from Toluca and sits east of the city in the municipality of Colón, next to the state's aerospace park. Private aircraft use the general aviation area, apart from the commercial terminal, and your car can meet you there. The historic center is about 30 minutes away by car, the main industrial parks 15 to 30, Tequisquiapan around 40, Peña de Bernal about 50 and San Miguel de Allende about 1 hour 15 minutes. This is a domestic flight, so there is no customs.

Documents for a private flight to Querétaro

Your flight to Querétaro is domestic, so Mexican nationals only need a valid official photo ID, such as an INE card or a passport. Foreign visitors should carry the passport they used to enter Mexico. Send the full name of every passenger on WhatsApp when you confirm, and the aircraft can be ready about 2 hours later. Customs and immigration do not apply on this route.

How long is a private jet flight from Mexico City to Querétaro?

About 35 minutes from Toluca (AIT) to Querétaro Intercontinental (QRO), roughly the length of a cross-town drive in Mexico City without the traffic. Add about an hour by car from Polanco, Santa Fe, Lomas or Interlomas to Toluca and under 10 minutes from the curb to the aircraft steps.

Is a private jet worth it for Querétaro?

For business, usually yes. Door-to-door time from Mexico City to Querétaro's industrial parks is under 2 hours by private jet, one to two hours faster than driving or the commercial alternatives, and the return can happen the same afternoon without a night in a hotel.

Can I continue to San Miguel de Allende from Querétaro?

Yes. San Miguel de Allende is about 1 hour 15 minutes by car from Querétaro Intercontinental, which makes QRO a convenient gateway for a weekend in Guanajuato. Tell JETCAB your final destination when you request the quote, so the flight time fits your plans.

Do you go through TSA when flying private?

TSA is the US screening agency, and it plays no part in a domestic flight from Toluca to Querétaro. At the Toluca private terminal there is a short ID check and baggage screening, then you walk to the aircraft. From car to aircraft steps takes under 10 minutes, with no lines and no gate.

Do you tip a private jet pilot?

It is never expected. Your JETCAB quote already covers the crew, along with fuel, catering and permits, so there is nothing extra to pay on the day. Some passengers leave a gratuity for the crew after a long weekend or a special occasion, and that is entirely your choice.
