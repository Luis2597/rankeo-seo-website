The trip

A city that runs on time

Monterrey is the industrial heart of northern Mexico, home to some of the country's largest companies in cement, steel, beer and banking, and the place where many of Mexico's biggest deals actually get signed. It sits in a bowl of mountains: Cerro de la Silla to the east, the Sierra Madre Oriental to the south and Cerro de las Mitras to the west. Look up from almost any street and there is a ridge in view, sharp against a hard blue sky.

The business address is San Pedro Garza García, polished and green, with glass towers rising along Valle Oriente and the shaded median of Calzada del Valle. Downtown, the Macroplaza runs past the cathedral and the orange laser tower of the Faro del Comercio, and the old steel foundry at Parque Fundidora is now a park of blast furnaces, museums and concert halls. In the evening, regiomontanos eat cabrito and arrachera, or stroll the Paseo Santa Lucía, a canal lined with restaurants.

At your destination

Where your private jet to Monterrey will take you

Best time to fly to Monterrey

Business travel to Monterrey runs year-round, and a private jet to Monterrey flies in every season. The climate is what to plan around. Summers are hot, with June to August highs often above 35 degrees Celsius, so early meetings and short outdoor plans work best. October through April brings mild days and cool nights, ideal for golf or a hike at Chipinque, although winter cold fronts can drop temperatures sharply for a day or two. Large conventions at Cintermex and spring events at Parque Fundidora raise hotel demand, so confirm your aircraft early for those weeks.

Arriving in Monterrey: MTY or Del Norte (NTR)

JETCAB flies to both of the city's airports, about 920 km from Toluca. Monterrey International (MTY), in Apodaca northeast of the city, has general aviation facilities separate from the commercial terminals and is about 30 to 45 minutes from San Pedro depending on traffic. Del Norte International (NTR), north of the city in General Escobedo, is focused on private and executive aviation and is a favorite with business travelers who want a quiet arrival. This is a domestic flight, so there is no customs at either airport.

Documents for a private flight to Monterrey

Your flight to Monterrey is domestic, so Mexican nationals only need a valid official photo ID, such as an INE card or a passport. Foreign visitors should carry the passport they used to enter Mexico. Send the full name of every passenger on WhatsApp when you confirm, and the aircraft can be ready about 2 hours later. Customs and immigration do not apply on this route.
