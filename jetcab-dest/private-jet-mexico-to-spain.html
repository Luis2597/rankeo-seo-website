The trip

An overnight crossing to the Old World

Spain runs on a different clock. Lunch starts at two, dinner at ten, and in Madrid the terraces of La Latina are still full past midnight. Barcelona pairs Gothic lanes with the Sagrada Família and a city beach a few metro stops away. In the south, Marbella's Golden Mile and the white villages of Andalusia trade city energy for sea light, and from May to October Ibiza fills its coves, beach clubs and farmhouse restaurants with people who planned the trip all year.

Getting there is usually the tiring part. Commercial flights from Mexico City mean fixed departure times, a crowded terminal and a long passport line at Barajas. A private jet to Spain flies nonstop in about 11 hours, so you can leave Toluca after an early dinner, sleep on a fully reclined seat and land in Madrid in the early afternoon. Families keep their own rhythm, and executives arrive rested enough to go straight into a meeting.

Door to door

Your overnight flight from Polanco to Madrid

  1. 6:30 pm

    Leave Polanco

    Your driver collects you in the early evening for the 45 to 60 minute drive west to Toluca. With less time to spare, the JETCAB helicopter transfer from Polanco, Lomas, Santa Fe or Reforma takes about 25 minutes.

  2. 7:45 pm

    Boarding at Toluca FBO

    Car to aircraft steps in under 10 minutes. Mexican exit immigration is handled at the private terminal, your luggage goes into the hold, and the crew settles you into the cabin for the night ahead.

  3. 8:00 pm

    Dinner, then sleep

    Wheels up. Dinner is served as the jet climbs northeast across the Gulf of Mexico toward the Atlantic. Then the lights go down, the seats recline fully and you sleep through most of the 11-hour crossing.

  4. 2:00 pm

    Landing in Madrid

    You land at Madrid-Barajas around 2:00 pm local time in winter; Spain is seven hours ahead of Mexico City in winter and eight in summer. Passport control and customs are handled through the business aviation terminal.

  5. 3:00 pm

    Late lunch in Salamanca

    The Salamanca district, Madrid's most elegant shopping neighborhood, is about 20 to 30 minutes away. Check in, walk Calle Serrano and sit down to a late lunch, which is right on schedule in Madrid. Dinner starts around ten.

At your destination

What to do in Spain after you land

When to fly a private jet to Spain

Spring and autumn are ideal for the cities: April to June and September to October bring mild days in Madrid and Barcelona and fewer crowds. Madrid celebrates San Isidro in mid-May, and Seville's April Fair follows a couple of weeks after Easter. July and August are hot inland and peak season on the coast, when Marbella and Ibiza are at their liveliest. Winter is quiet and good for museums and shopping. Transatlantic flights need 48 to 72 hours for overflight permits, and for summer weekends on the islands, reserve your aircraft several weeks ahead.

Arriving in Madrid or Barcelona

JETCAB flies to Madrid-Barajas (MAD) and Barcelona El Prat (BCN). Both airports have business aviation terminals where private passengers clear passport control and customs away from the main halls, with the handling arranged by JETCAB before departure. From Barajas, the Salamanca district and central Madrid are about 20 to 30 minutes by car. From El Prat, Passeig de Gràcia is roughly 25 minutes away. For Marbella or Ibiza, ask for a quote to Málaga or Ibiza airport, and your car can be waiting at the terminal door.

Documents for flying private to Spain

Mexican and US citizens currently enter Spain without a visa for short stays of up to 90 days in any 180-day period, with a passport valid at least three months beyond the date they leave the Schengen area. The EU's biometric Entry/Exit System now registers non-EU travelers at the border, and the ETIAS travel authorization is due to begin, so check its status before you fly. JETCAB handles overflight and landing permits, which take 48 to 72 hours.

Questions

Spain private jet FAQ

How long is a private jet flight from Mexico City to Spain?

About 11 hours nonstop from Toluca (AIT) to Madrid (MAD) or Barcelona (BCN), a distance of roughly 9,200 km, on a long-range Gulfstream GV or Global Express. Leaving Toluca at 8:00 pm puts you in Madrid in the early afternoon, local time, after a night's sleep on board.

How much does a private jet to Spain cost?

A private jet to Spain from Mexico City starts at $100,000 USD one way for the whole aircraft, on a Gulfstream GV or Global Express for up to 16 passengers. Crew, fuel, catering, permits and handling are included. Return legs and other aircraft are quoted within 30 minutes on WhatsApp.

Are private jets allowed to fly internationally?

Yes. Private jets cross borders every day, provided the operator holds the right authorizations and files the permits each country requires. JETCAB has operated since 1999 under an AFAC operating permit and arranges the overflight and landing clearances for transatlantic flights, which is why Spain needs 48 to 72 hours of lead time.

Which aircraft fly nonstop from Mexico to Spain?

Long-range jets such as the Gulfstream GV and the Global Express, which can cross the Atlantic from Toluca without a fuel stop. Both carry up to 16 passengers with reclining seats, a galley for proper meals and space to sleep through the night before landing in Madrid or Barcelona.

Can I fly private to Ibiza or Marbella from Mexico?

Ask for it when you request your quote. Ibiza has its own international airport, and Málaga is the closest major airport to Marbella, about 45 minutes away by car. Flight time, aircraft and price for either route are confirmed on WhatsApp within 30 minutes, permits included.

Do you tip a private jet pilot?

Tipping is not required, and no gratuity is ever added to a JETCAB quote since the price already covers the crew. On a long overnight crossing, if the crew went out of their way for your family, a tip is a personal gesture left entirely to you.
