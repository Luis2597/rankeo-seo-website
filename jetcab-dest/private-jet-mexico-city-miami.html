The trip

Breakfast in Polanco, lunch in Brickell

For many families in Mexico City, Miami is a second home. The bank is in Brickell, the apartment overlooks Biscayne Bay, the kids know the beach at Key Biscayne better than any in Mexico. Getting there used to mean a morning flight out of the capital's main airport, a long immigration line at MIA and an hour in traffic on the Dolphin Expressway. A private jet to Miami trims all of that down to a quiet morning and a nonstop flight across the Gulf of Mexico.

You land at Opa-locka Executive, north of downtown, and clear US Customs inside the private terminal in minutes. Twenty minutes later you are crossing the causeway to South Beach, windows down, the water a shade of green that never looks real in photographs. By early afternoon you can be at lunch in the Design District or on a lounger in Mid-Beach. On Sunday, the same aircraft flies you home, and Monday starts in Mexico City as planned.

    Over the causeway

    Twenty minutes later you are crossing Biscayne Bay to South Beach, or 25 minutes south to Brickell. Drop your bags, change into something lighter, and walk out to lunch while the afternoon is still young.

At your destination

What to do in Miami after your private jet lands

Best time to fly private to Miami

Miami's high season runs from December to April, when the air is dry, the sea is calm and daytime temperatures sit in the mid to upper 70s Fahrenheit. It is also when the city fills up. Art Basel Miami Beach takes over the first week of December, the Miami Open tennis tournament arrives in March, and the Formula 1 Miami Grand Prix brings huge crowds in May. Stone crab season runs from October 15 to May 1. Hurricane season lasts from June 1 to November 30, with afternoon storms most summer days. For holiday weeks and event weekends, book your aircraft and hotel well ahead.

Miami-Opa Locka Executive (OPF): arriving in Miami

JETCAB flies to Miami-Opa Locka Executive Airport (OPF), north of downtown Miami. It is about 20 minutes from South Beach and 25 minutes from Brickell, and it serves private and corporate aircraft away from the crowds at Miami International. US Customs and Border Protection clears international arrivals inside the FBO, usually within minutes, using the eAPIS manifest JETCAB files about 24 hours before departure. Your bags stay with the aircraft until they go into your car, and your driver can wait just outside the private terminal.

Documents for a private flight to Miami

Every passenger needs a valid passport, and Mexican citizens also need a valid US visa. Travelers of other nationalities need the entry document their passport requires for the United States. JETCAB files the eAPIS manifest with US Customs and Border Protection, so we need passenger names, dates of birth and passport details about 24 hours before departure. Officers check documents at the FBO when you land.

Questions

Miami private jet FAQ

How long is a private jet flight from Mexico City to Miami?

About 3 hours 30 minutes nonstop from Toluca International Airport (TLC) to Miami-Opa Locka Executive (OPF). Add 45 to 60 minutes by car from Polanco to Toluca, or about 25 by helicopter, and roughly 20 minutes from Opa-locka to South Beach.

How much does a private jet to Miami cost?

From Mexico City, a private jet to Miami starts at $4,800 USD one way on a Learjet 35 for up to 7 passengers, $9,500 on a Challenger 605 for 12 and $18,000 on a Gulfstream GV for 16. Prices are per aircraft and include crew, fuel, catering and permits.

Is Miami Executive Airport for private jets?

Yes. Miami Executive Airport (TMB), in Kendall, southwest of the city, is a general aviation airport used by private aircraft. For flights from Mexico City, JETCAB lands at Miami-Opa Locka Executive (OPF), north of downtown, where US Customs clears passengers inside the FBO and South Beach is about 20 minutes away.

What documents do I need to fly private to Miami?

A valid passport for every passenger and, for Mexican citizens, a valid US visa. JETCAB needs passenger details about 24 hours before departure to file the eAPIS manifest with US Customs. Officers check your documents inside the private terminal at Opa-locka when you land.

Do you tip a private jet pilot?

Tipping is not expected, because crew costs are already part of the charter price. If the pilots and crew made the trip especially easy, a thank-you note or a gratuity handed to the captain is a kind gesture. It is always optional, and nobody will expect it.

What is the cheapest way to fly private to Miami?

The Learjet 35 is the most affordable option at $4,800 USD one way for up to 7 passengers, which comes to under $700 per seat with a full cabin. Since you pay for the whole aircraft, flying with family or colleagues is the easiest way to bring the cost per person down.
