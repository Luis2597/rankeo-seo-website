The trip

A beach town that kept its soul

Puerto Vallarta grew up as a fishing village, and the old town has kept its bones: white walls, bougainvillea, steep cobblestone lanes and the crown on top of the church of Our Lady of Guadalupe. The Malecón runs along the water, lined with bronze sculptures, and every evening people lean on the seawall to watch the sun drop into Banderas Bay. Hollywood put the town on the map in the 1960s, and a little of that old glamour never left.

The bay is one of the largest in Mexico, and it shapes everything here. South of town, the coastal road winds past hidden coves and the granite islets of Los Arcos toward Mismaloya. North, the shore curves into Riviera Nayarit: Nuevo Vallarta, Bucerías and finally Punta Mita, where the villas and golf courses sit. Behind it all, the Sierra Madre rises green and close, with jungle restaurants and river pools a short drive up the valleys.

    The flight heads west across Michoacán and Jalisco, over volcanic highlands and mountain lakes. Halfway there the ridges start to fall away toward the coast, and the Pacific shows up as a bright line on the horizon.

  4. 5:00 pm

    Landing at PVR

    You land at Puerto Vallarta International after about 1 hour 45 minutes. Jalisco shares Mexico City's time zone, so the clock stays the same. Your car is waiting on the private aviation side of the airport.

    Sunset on the bay

    Fifteen minutes to the Marina or Nuevo Vallarta, about forty-five to Punta Mita. The kids are in the pool before dark, and you have a margarita in hand when the sun slides into Banderas Bay.

At your destination

What to do in Puerto Vallarta after you land

Best time to fly to Puerto Vallarta

November through April is the classic season in Puerto Vallarta, with dry, sunny days around 28 degrees Celsius, cool evenings and calm water. From December to March, humpback whales fill Banderas Bay to breed and give birth, and boat tours go out every morning. Christmas, New Year and Semana Santa are the busiest weeks, so confirm your aircraft early for those dates. May is hot and quiet. June through October is the rainy season, with short tropical storms in the afternoon, higher humidity and the jungle at its greenest. The Pacific hurricane season runs from mid-May through November.

Arriving at Puerto Vallarta International (PVR)

Puerto Vallarta International (PVR), officially Licenciado Gustavo Díaz Ordaz International, is about 900 km from Toluca and sits on the north side of town, between Puerto Vallarta and Nuevo Vallarta. Private aircraft use the general aviation area, away from the commercial terminal, so you walk to your car without crossing the arrivals hall. The Marina is about 5 minutes away, Nuevo Vallarta 15, the Malecón and the Zona Romántica 20 to 25, and Punta Mita about 45 minutes. This is a domestic flight, so there is no customs or immigration.

Documents for a private flight to Puerto Vallarta

Your flight to Puerto Vallarta is domestic, so Mexican nationals only need a valid official photo ID, such as an INE card or a passport. Foreign visitors should carry the passport they used to enter Mexico. Send the full name of every passenger on WhatsApp when you confirm, and the aircraft can be ready about 2 hours later. Customs and immigration do not apply on this route.

Can I fly to Puerto Vallarta and back the same day?

Yes. At 1 hour 45 minutes each way, JETCAB regularly arranges same-day Puerto Vallarta itineraries for property visits, family events and business lunches. You can leave Toluca in the morning, spend the day on the bay and be back home that night.

Can you fly a private plane into Mexico?

Yes, although foreign aircraft need landing permits and other paperwork. A JETCAB flight from Toluca to Puerto Vallarta is a domestic operation under JETCAB's AFAC operating permit, so there is no international paperwork at all. Each passenger only needs an official photo ID.

When is whale season in Puerto Vallarta?

Humpback whales arrive in Banderas Bay from about December to March to breed and give birth. Boat tours leave from the Marina and from Punta Mita, and on calm days you can often see whales breaching from the shore. It is also the most popular season to fly in.
