The trip

Where the Pacific does the convincing

San Diego moves at the speed of the tide. Mornings start gray and cool along the coast, the marine layer lifts by late morning, and by afternoon the light over Mission Bay turns gold. Surfers paddle out at Windansea, sailboats tack past Point Loma, and the downtown towers glow against the harbor. It is a city of neighborhoods with distinct moods: the Victorian storefronts of the Gaslamp Quarter, the cafés of Little Italy, the bluffs of La Jolla where harbor seals claim the beach.

Flying private changes the shape of the trip. The commercial options from Mexico City mean airline schedules, terminal lines, or a drive through the Tijuana crossing, where car waits can stretch for hours. With a private jet to San Diego, you leave Toluca after breakfast and land mid-morning on the West Coast, since San Diego runs one to two hours behind Mexico City. A board meeting on the Torrey Pines mesa, a specialist appointment or a long weekend in Coronado all fit inside one easy day.

At your destination

What to do in San Diego after you land

When to fly to San Diego

San Diego is mild all year, with daytime highs in the mid 60s°F in winter and the mid 70s in summer. May and June bring the coastal fog locals call May Gray and June Gloom, while September and October are often the warmest, clearest weeks at the beach. From December to April, gray whales migrate past Point Loma. July is the busiest month: Comic-Con fills downtown hotels and the Del Mar racing season opens with its famous hats. For those dates, request your quote a week or more ahead. The rest of the year, US flights need about 24 hours.

Arriving at San Diego International (SAN)

San Diego International sits on the bay about three miles from downtown, one of the closest big-city airports to its center in the country. Private aircraft use the general aviation side, away from the commercial terminals. Before departure JETCAB files the eAPIS manifest and books your US Customs clearance, so formalities on arrival are handled for your party alone. Downtown and Little Italy are about 10 minutes by car, Coronado about 15 and La Jolla about 20. For Carlsbad, Del Mar or Rancho Santa Fe, McClellan-Palomar Airport (CRQ) puts you closer to North County.

Documents for a private jet to San Diego

Mexican citizens need a valid passport and a valid US visa. US citizens travel on their passport, and permanent residents on their green card. JETCAB files the eAPIS manifest with US Customs, so send each passenger's full name, date of birth and passport number about 24 hours before departure. Children need their own passports. If you plan to drive across into Baja during the trip, keep the same documents with you for the return crossing.

San Diego private jet FAQ

How long is a private jet flight from Mexico City to San Diego?

About 3 hours 20 minutes nonstop from Toluca (AIT) to San Diego International (SAN), a distance of roughly 2,700 km. San Diego runs one to two hours behind Mexico City, so a flight leaving Toluca at 8:15 am lands mid-morning local time, with the whole day still ahead of you.

How much does a private jet to San Diego cost?

A private jet to San Diego from Mexico City starts at $9,000 USD one way for the full aircraft, with crew, fuel, catering and permits included. Larger cabins such as the Challenger 605 or Gulfstream GV are quoted in 30 minutes on WhatsApp, and the exact price is confirmed before you book.

Do I have to cross the Tijuana border to reach San Diego?

No. Your jet flies straight to San Diego, so you skip the San Ysidro and Otay Mesa road crossings entirely. You still enter the United States formally: JETCAB files the eAPIS manifest ahead of time, and customs clears your party on arrival with your passport and visa.

Which airport do private jets use in San Diego?

San Diego International (SAN) is the closest to downtown, about 10 minutes away. McClellan-Palomar Airport (CRQ) in Carlsbad suits trips to North County, Del Mar and Rancho Santa Fe. JETCAB confirms which airport fits your plans, your aircraft and the customs arrival when it sends the quote.

Do you tip a private jet pilot?

Tipping is never part of the JETCAB quote and it is not required. The price already covers the crew. If a pilot or flight attendant went out of their way for your family, a gratuity is a personal gesture left entirely to you, and a sincere thank you on the steps is always welcome.

What is the cheapest way to fly privately to San Diego?

Fill the seats. Because the price covers the whole aircraft, a Learjet 35 carrying seven passengers turns $9,000 USD into a much smaller share per person. Flexible dates help too: when you request a quote, ask about empty legs, the repositioning flights that sometimes match a route.
