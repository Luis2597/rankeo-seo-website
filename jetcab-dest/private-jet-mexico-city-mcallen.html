The trip

South Texas, an hour and a half away

McAllen sits in the Rio Grande Valley, where citrus groves and palm trees line the roads and Spanish is heard as often as English. For families from Monterrey, Mexico City and beyond, it has long been the familiar first stop in Texas: a weekend of shopping, a medical consultation, a closing with a bank or a broker. The city is compact and easy, with wide streets, good steakhouses and winter afternoons warm enough to eat outside.

Flying private shrinks the trip to almost nothing. A private jet to McAllen covers about 900 km in an hour and a half, which makes a same-day round trip practical for an appointment at a hospital in Edinburg or a meeting with a cross-border logistics partner. You land, clear US Customs at the airport with only your own group around you, and reach La Plaza Mall or your doctor's office within minutes.

    Your driver picks you up early for the 45 to 60 minute drive west to Toluca. If you would rather skip the highway, the JETCAB helicopter transfer from Polanco, Lomas, Santa Fe or Reforma takes about 25 minutes.

  2. 8:00 am

    Boarding at Toluca FBO

    Car to aircraft steps in under 10 minutes at the private terminal. Your passport and visa details went into the eAPIS manifest the day before, so there is nothing to fill out here. Bags go into the hold and you take your seat.

  3. 8:15 am

    North over the Sierra

    The flight heads north toward the Texas border. Ninety minutes is enough for breakfast and a call, or simply for watching the Sierra Madre Oriental give way to the flat ranchland and farm fields of Tamaulipas below.

  4. 9:45 am

    Landing at MFE

    You land at McAllen International about 1 hour 30 minutes after takeoff. In winter the clock matches Mexico City, and in summer Texas is one hour ahead. US Customs clears your party at the airport, and your car is waiting.

  5. 10:15 am

    Into the city

    La Plaza Mall is about five minutes from the airport and downtown McAllen about ten. Start with your appointment or meeting, then have lunch at a steakhouse before an afternoon of shopping or the short drive to Edinburg.

At your destination

What to do in McAllen after you land

When to fly to McAllen

McAllen is subtropical. From June to September afternoons regularly pass 100°F, so plan outdoor time for the mornings. October through April is the comfortable season, with winter highs in the 70s, when Winter Texans arrive from the Midwest and birders come for migrating species. The McAllen Holiday Parade in early December is one of the largest in Texas, and the Christmas shopping season keeps hotels busy. Spring break fills South Padre Island. US flights need about 24 hours for the eAPIS manifest, so book a day ahead whenever you can.

Arriving at McAllen International (MFE)

McAllen International Airport, long known as McAllen Miller International, sits about two miles south of downtown. US Customs and Border Protection officers clear international private flights on arrival, and JETCAB files the eAPIS manifest ahead of time and coordinates your arrival with the private terminal. La Plaza Mall and the main hotel district are about five minutes away, the medical district in Edinburg around 20 minutes, and South Padre Island roughly an hour and a half by car. Same-day round trips are simple to plan from here.

Documents for a private jet to McAllen

Mexican citizens need a valid passport and a valid US visa. US citizens travel on their passport, and permanent residents on their green card. JETCAB files the eAPIS manifest with US Customs, so send each passenger's full name, date of birth and passport number about 24 hours before departure. Children need their own documents. For medical trips, carry any records or test results your doctor asked for in your hand luggage.

What is the name of the airport in McAllen, Texas?

McAllen International Airport, IATA code MFE, known for years as McAllen Miller International. It sits about two miles south of downtown and clears international private flights through US Customs, which makes it the natural landing point for a private jet to McAllen from Mexico.

Can I fly to McAllen and back the same day?

Yes. At about 1 hour 30 minutes each way, a same-day round trip is easy to plan around a medical appointment, a closing or a day of shopping. Tell us your return time when you request the quote and JETCAB prices both legs together.

What is an empty leg?

An empty leg is a flight an aircraft makes without passengers, usually to reposition after dropping someone off. When one happens to match your route and date, it can be offered at a lower price. The timing is set by the original trip, so it suits travelers with flexible plans.

Do you tip a private jet pilot?

Tipping is not required, and no gratuity is ever added to a JETCAB quote because the price already covers the crew. If a pilot or flight attendant went out of their way for a patient or a family member, a tip is a personal gesture left to you.
