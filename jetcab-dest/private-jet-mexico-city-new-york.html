The trip

Manhattan, on your schedule

The meeting is Thursday at nine on Park Avenue. On a commercial ticket, that means a dawn departure, a connection in Houston or Dallas and a taxi line at JFK. On a private jet to New York, you leave Toluca after breakfast on Wednesday, work or sleep across the Gulf and the Carolinas, and land at Teterboro, across the Hudson in New Jersey, in time to check in before dinner. The skyline appears on the descent like a cutout against the late light.

Teterboro is where New York's private aviation lives. There are no airline gates and no terminal crowds, only FBOs and a short drive through the Lincoln Tunnel to Midtown, about 20 minutes when traffic allows. That gives you the evening for a table in the West Village or a walk along the High Line, and the morning fresh for the meeting you actually came for. When it closes, the same aircraft is ready to fly you back to Mexico City.

At your destination

What to do in New York after your private jet lands

Best time to fly private to New York

New York is at its best in two windows. From late April to early June, the parks bloom and outdoor dining fills the sidewalks; the Met Gala lands on the first Monday in May. From mid-September to early November, the air turns crisp and Central Park's foliage peaks in late October. Summer is hot and humid, and many New Yorkers leave for the Hamptons on weekends. December brings the Rockefeller Center tree and holiday windows, along with the year's highest hotel rates. January and February can bring snowstorms that delay arrivals. During UN General Assembly week in September, Midtown traffic slows to a crawl, so book early and allow extra time.

Teterboro (TEB): New York's private jet airport

JETCAB flies to Teterboro Airport (TEB), in Bergen County, New Jersey, about 20 minutes from Midtown Manhattan through the Lincoln Tunnel when traffic is light. Teterboro has no scheduled airline service, so it is used almost entirely by private and corporate aircraft, and passengers board and arrive through FBOs instead of a terminal. JFK is also available on request. US Customs and Border Protection clears you at your first US landing, at the private terminal, using the eAPIS manifest JETCAB files about 24 hours before departure. On the Learjet 35, the route includes one fuel stop.

Documents for a private flight to New York

Every passenger needs a valid passport. Mexican citizens also need a valid US visa, and travelers of other nationalities need whatever entry document their passport requires for the United States. JETCAB files the eAPIS manifest with US Customs and Border Protection, so passenger names, dates of birth and passport details are needed about 24 hours before departure. Keep your passport handy on board, since officers check documents when you land.

Questions

New York private jet FAQ

How long is a private jet flight from Mexico City to New York?

About 5 hours 30 minutes from Toluca International Airport (TLC) to Teterboro (TEB), roughly 3,100 km. The Learjet 35 makes one fuel stop on the way. For the most direct private jet to New York, ask about the Challenger 605 or the Gulfstream GV when you request your quote.

How much does a private jet to New York cost?

From Mexico City, a private jet to New York starts at $9,500 USD one way on a Learjet 35 for up to 7 passengers, $18,500 on a Challenger 605 for 12 and $28,000 on a Gulfstream GV for 16. Prices are per aircraft and include crew, fuel, catering, permits and US customs filings.

Is Teterboro a private airport?

Teterboro is a public-use airport owned by the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey, but it has no scheduled airline service. Nearly all its traffic is private and corporate aircraft, which is why it works so well for Manhattan: about 20 minutes to Midtown and no commercial terminal at all.

Do you tip a private jet pilot?

Tipping is not expected. Crew costs are already part of the charter price, so nothing extra is required. If a crew went out of their way for you, a thank-you note or a gratuity handed to the captain is a kind gesture, and it is always optional.

What documents do I need to fly private to New York?

A valid passport for every passenger and, for Mexican citizens, a valid US visa. JETCAB needs passenger details about 24 hours before departure to file the eAPIS manifest with US Customs. Officers check documents when you land, at the private terminal of your first US airport.

What is the cheapest way to fly privately to New York?

The Learjet 35 is the most affordable option, from $9,500 USD one way for up to 7 passengers, which comes to under $1,400 per seat when the cabin is full. It makes one fuel stop. Sharing the aircraft with family or colleagues is the simplest way to lower the cost per person.
