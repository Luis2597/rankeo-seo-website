Can you fly a private plane from the US to Mexico?

You can, and the cleanest way is to charter with an operator that holds a permit from Mexico's civil aviation authority, the AFAC. JETCAB is an AFAC permit holder with more than 2,000 flights and more than 200 destinations across Mexico, the United States, Latin America and Europe.

A charter means you rent the whole aircraft. The quote covers crew, fuel, catering and permits, and the price is the same whether one person flies or the cabin is full. The operator files the flight plan, sends the passenger data to the authorities and arranges the arrival slot.

Pilots who want to fly their own aircraft across the border take on that paperwork themselves. The requirements on the US side are published by CBP for private flyers, and the Mexican side is governed by the AFAC. If you are a passenger rather than a pilot, the operator handles all of it.

What documents do you need to fly private between the US and Mexico?

Every passenger needs a valid passport. Travelers who are not US citizens or residents also need a valid B1/B2 visa to enter the United States.

One point surprises many travelers. The ESTA, which lets citizens of some countries visit the US under the Visa Waiver Program, does not apply on private aircraft. A traveler who would normally fly commercial on an ESTA needs a visa to arrive by private jet.

For entry into Mexico, requirements depend on nationality. Mexico's immigration institute, the INM, publishes the current rules, and it is worth checking them before you send your passenger list.

Do you have to go through customs on a private jet?

Yes. Every private flight that crosses the border clears immigration and customs at the arrival airport. What changes is where and how long it takes.

Arriving in the US, CBP officers process passengers at the private terminal of the arrival airport. Arriving in Mexico, immigration and customs also take place at the private terminal. Either way, you skip the passport hall of a commercial terminal and the long lines that go with it.

The departure side is just as short. At Toluca, the Tarmac-to-Cabin process takes passengers from the car to the airstair in under 10 minutes. How screening works on a private flight in each country is covered in our guide on whether you go through security on a private jet.

How far in advance should you book a flight to the US?

Plan on about 24 hours for a flight from Mexico to the United States. That window covers the eAPIS notice, the electronic passenger manifest US authorities require before departure, and the arrival appointment with CBP.

Inside Mexico, an aircraft can be ready in 2 hours. Flights to other international destinations take 48 to 72 hours because they need overflight permits from the countries along the route.

The fastest way to keep that 24-hour window intact is to send complete passenger data with the request. Full names as they appear on passports, dates of birth, passport numbers and nationalities are what the manifest needs. If one detail is missing, the clock waits for it.
