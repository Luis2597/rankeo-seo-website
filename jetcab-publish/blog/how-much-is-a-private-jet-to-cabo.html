What does a private jet to Cabo include?

The quoted price includes the flight crew, fuel, catering and the permits for the trip. There is no separate charge per passenger and no seat fee.

Ground time in Toluca is short. The Tarmac-to-Cabin boarding process takes you from your car to the airstair in under 10 minutes, with no public terminal in between. Every crew member signs a non-disclosure agreement for each flight, and passenger data is never shared. For families and executives who would rather not be photographed walking through a commercial concourse in shorts, that matters as much as the flight time.

Pets ride in the cabin with you. Luggage limits depend on the aircraft. If you are bringing golf bags or fishing gear, list them when you ask for the quote and the right cabin will be assigned.

Which jet should you pick for your group?

For up to 7 passengers, a light jet such as the Learjet 35 or Learjet 45 is the efficient choice for Cabo. Above that number, or when you want to stand up and move around for two and a half hours, step up to a midsize.

  • Learjet 35 and Learjet 45. Light jets for up to 7 passengers. The Learjet 35 carries the published 3,800 USD fare.
  • Hawker 800. A midsize jet with a roomier cabin for the same kind of trip.
  • Challenger 605. Midsize with a stand-up cabin and seating for up to 12. A strong fit for two families traveling together.
  • Gulfstream GV. Long range, up to 16 passengers. On a domestic leg like Cabo it makes sense for large groups or for travelers who want the biggest cabin available.

A useful rule of thumb: count passengers first, then luggage. A group of six with soft bags fits a light jet well. A group of six with surfboards and a week of luggage may be happier in the Hawker.

How much is a round trip private jet to Cabo?

A round trip is quoted as its own trip, and the total depends on how long you stay. The 3,800 USD figure covers a single leg, and what happens between the two legs shapes the final number.

There are two common setups. If you stay a night or two, the aircraft and crew can wait in Los Cabos, which adds an overnight for the crew to the quote. If you stay a week, it usually makes more sense for the jet to fly back to Toluca and return to pick you up. That means repositioning flights on both ends. JETCAB prices both options so you can compare them side by side.

Flexibility on dates opens a third path. When an aircraft has to fly empty to or from Cabo for another client, that empty leg is offered at up to 30% below the regular fare. How far ahead do you need to book?

Cabo is a domestic flight, so JETCAB can have an aircraft ready in 2 hours for most requests. The quote itself comes back closed in 30 minutes.

Peak weeks fill up faster. Holiday periods and long weekends see more demand for the same aircraft, so booking a few days out gives you the best pick of cabins. Last-minute trips still work, though you choose from what is available that day.

If you are an international visitor connecting through Mexico City, the private flight to Cabo does not require you to change terminals inside a commercial airport. You drive to Toluca, about 65 km from the city, and board from the private terminal.
