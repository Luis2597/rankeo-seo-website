How much does a 2-hour private jet flight cost per person?

Divide the aircraft price by the number of passengers. A Learjet 35 to Cancún at 3,200 USD works out to about 457 USD per person with 7 on board, and 800 USD each with 4. A Challenger 605 on the same route, at 6,500 USD with 12 passengers, comes to about 542 USD each.

That math is why groups and families get the most value from a private charter. The fare stays the same whether the cabin is full or half empty. If you are traveling alone, a 2-hour flight is still the price of the whole jet.

Why doesn't the hourly rate match the final quote?

Because the hourly rate covers only time in the air, and a real trip has more moving parts. The route fare already folds those in, which is why it is the better number to plan with.

Three things shift a quote. The first is repositioning: if the aircraft has to fly empty to pick you up or return to base, that time is part of the job. The second is a round trip with a wait. If the crew stays with the aircraft at your destination, the quote reflects the waiting time and any overnight stay. The third is the aircraft you choose. A stand-up cabin in a Challenger 605 costs more per hour than a Learjet, and on a 2-hour leg the difference shows.

Is there a cheaper way to take a 2-hour private flight?

Yes, if your dates are flexible. Empty legs are flights where an aircraft returns to base without passengers after dropping off another client. JETCAB sells them at up to 30 % below the regular fare and announces them on its private WhatsApp list 24 to 72 hours ahead.

The trade-off is control. The original trip sets the route and the departure window, so empty legs suit travelers who can move a day or pick between two destinations. If your date is fixed, the published fare is the reliable path.

What does a 2-hour private flight look like on the day?

You drive to JETCAB's private terminal (FBO) at Toluca, and you are in your seat in under 10 minutes. There is no public terminal on the way. The crew signs a nondisclosure agreement on every flight, and passenger data is never shared.

For domestic routes like Cancún or Puerto Vallarta, the aircraft can be ready 2 hours after you confirm. For Houston, plan on about 24 hours, the time needed to file eAPIS and coordinate with US Customs and Border Protection. Each passenger needs a valid passport and a US visa. ESTA does not apply to private aircraft, and immigration and customs are handled at the private terminal where you land. The CBP guidance for private flyers covers the US side in detail.

Pets fly in the cabin with you. Catering is part of the fare. Payment is by bank transfer in MXN or USD with a Mexican CFDI invoice, or by credit card depending on the amount.
