How can you lower the cost of a private jet?

The most effective lever is choosing the smallest aircraft that fits your group and your route. A Learjet 35 seats seven, so a group of five gains nothing from a larger cabin on a 50-minute flight to Guadalajara.

Filling the cabin changes the math per person. Since you pay per aircraft, seven passengers splitting a Toluca to Monterrey flight at 2,200 USD pay a fraction of what one traveler pays alone.

Empty legs are the other route to a lower price. When an aircraft has to reposition, that leg can sell for up to 30 percent below the normal rate. JETCAB announces them on a private WhatsApp list 24 to 72 hours ahead. If your dates are flexible, ask to join.

Is it cheaper to own a jet or charter one?

For occasional flying, charter usually costs less, because an owner pays for crew, hangar, insurance and maintenance whether the aircraft flies or not. Ownership starts to make sense with heavy, steady use all year. Charter also lets you pick a different aircraft for each trip.
