Is there a private airport in Mexico City?

There is no airport inside Mexico City reserved only for private aviation. The city's main airport, MEX, is built around airline schedules. Private aircraft can use it, but they share the airspace, taxiways and ground handling with a large commercial operation.

In practice, the private jet airport for Mexico City is Toluca. TLC (ICAO code MMTO) is an international airport west of the capital with private terminals, known in the industry as FBOs (fixed base operators). JETCAB keeps its aircraft and crews there, and departures run on the passenger's schedule. When someone says they are flying private out of Mexico City, the departure is very often Toluca.

When does Mexico City airport make more sense?

MEX makes sense mainly when your trip starts or ends with an airline flight that uses it. If you land at MEX on a commercial flight and want to continue privately without crossing the city, ask about it when you request the quote. The team will tell you what is possible for that date and how it affects the price.

For almost every other private trip, Toluca is the cleaner option. JETCAB's aircraft and crews are already on the ramp there, and you board from a private terminal at the hour you choose. A busy airline hub works on its own clock.

What if you are flying to Toluca from the United States?

Toluca is an international airport, so a private flight from the US can land there directly and put you 65 km from Mexico City. Before you fly, check the entry requirements for your nationality with Mexico's immigration authority, the INM. For the full picture on US to Mexico rules, documents and customs, read our guide on whether you can fly a private plane from the US to Mexico.

Heading north, Mexican passengers need a valid passport and a B1/B2 visa. ESTA does not apply to private aircraft. Immigration and customs are handled at the private terminal where you land.
