Which aircraft is right for Miami?

The right aircraft comes down to group size and how much cabin you want for three and a half hours. All three jets in the table fly the route, so the choice is about comfort and passengers.

The Learjet 35 suits couples, small families and executives traveling with one or two colleagues. It carries 7 passengers at the lowest fare on the route. The Challenger 605 is the step up most groups take for a flight of this length. Its stand-up cabin lets you move around, work at a table or rest, and it seats up to 12. The Gulfstream GV carries up to 16 and is the aircraft for large families or delegations who want everyone on one flight.

Pets fly in the cabin with you on every JETCAB aircraft, which matters for families who split the year between Mexico and South Florida.

Why does the flight use Opa-locka instead of Miami International?

OPF is an executive airport in the Miami area, which makes it a better fit for private aircraft than the region's main airline hub. You land, clear immigration and customs at the private terminal, and leave without passing through a commercial terminal.

On the Mexican side, the equivalent is Toluca. JETCAB is based at Toluca's private terminal (FBO), 65 km from Mexico City. Your car pulls up close to the aircraft, and you are in your seat in under 10 minutes.

What documents do you need to fly private to Miami?

Mexican passengers need a valid passport and a B1/B2 visa. That is the same visa used on a commercial flight. Many travelers miss one detail. ESTA does not apply to private aircraft. If you hold a passport from a Visa Waiver country and planned to travel on ESTA, check the rules on CBP's Visa Waiver Program page before you book.

The operator handles the filings. JETCAB submits the eAPIS manifest with each passenger's passport details and coordinates arrival with US Customs and Border Protection. That is why a Miami flight needs about 24 hours of lead time, compared with 2 hours for a domestic departure. CBP explains the process for private flyers on its site.
