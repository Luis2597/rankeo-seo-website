Do you still go through TSA on a private jet?

From Toluca, no. The Transportation Security Administration is a US agency, and its checkpoints serve airline passengers at commercial terminals in the United States. A JETCAB flight departs from a private terminal (FBO) at Toluca International Airport, 65 km from Mexico City, so there is no TSA lane anywhere in the process.

On charters departing US airports, the answer depends on the aircraft and the type of operation. Private terminals do not run the airline checkpoint, but security programs can still apply to some charter operations. TSA publishes its general screening rules on its security screening page. The practical takeaway for passengers is simple. On a private flight, the operator is responsible for knowing who is on board, and it will verify that before the doors close.

What security checks happen when you fly private from Toluca?

The checks are built around the passenger list. When you confirm a flight, JETCAB collects each traveler's full name and document details. On the day, the team at the private terminal matches each passenger against that list before boarding. Luggage goes to the aircraft with you.

That is the whole sequence for a domestic flight, and it is why Tarmac-to-Cabin boarding takes under 10 minutes from the moment your car arrives. You never cross a public terminal. Domestic aircraft can be ready 2 hours after confirmation.

Do you have to go through customs on a private jet?

Yes. Any international flight clears immigration and customs, private or not. What changes is where it happens and how long it takes. On a private flight, the officers meet the aircraft's passengers at the private terminal of arrival.

Flying into the United States, the operator files an eAPIS passenger notice before departure and CBP processes the passengers on landing. JETCAB plans US flights with about 24 hours of lead time for this step. CBP describes the process on its private flyers page.

The documents matter. Mexican passengers need a valid passport and a B1/B2 visa. ESTA does not apply on private aircraft. If you usually enter the US on an airline under the Visa Waiver Program, check the CBP Visa Waiver page before you book. Flying into Mexico, immigration and customs also take place at the private terminal of arrival.

What can you bring on a private jet?

What you can bring is set by the operator, within the rules for aviation safety. The airline liquids routine belongs to the commercial checkpoint. On a charter, the operator decides what goes on board and where, and regulations on hazardous materials still apply to every aircraft.

Tell the team in advance about anything unusual, such as sports equipment, large cases, medical supplies or high-value items. Space differs a great deal between a light jet and a larger aircraft. A Learjet 35 or Learjet 45 seats seven, a Challenger 605 up to 12 and a Gulfstream GV up to 16, and luggage space follows cabin size. Pets fly in the cabin with their owners.

Can you use the bathroom on a private jet?

That depends on the aircraft. Lavatory layout varies by model, and a compact light jet is laid out differently from a midsize jet with a stand-up cabin like the Challenger 605. If it matters for your trip, ask when you request the quote and the aircraft will be matched to it.

It matters most on longer legs. Toluca to Houston takes 2 hours 45 minutes, to Miami 3 hours 30 minutes and to Los Angeles 3 hours 45 minutes.
