How much is it to hire a helicopter for 2 hours?

Hiring a helicopter for 2 hours costs from 3,000 USD in a Bell 206 and from 7,000 USD in an AW139. The key detail is what counts as those two hours.

Billed time is the time the helicopter spends flying for your trip. That includes the leg out to pick you up if your departure point is away from its base, and the return if it flies home empty. Operators call those legs repositioning. A two-hour quote can mean ninety minutes with you on board and thirty minutes of positioning, or the full two hours in the air together.

Ground time matters too. A one-way drop costs less than a round trip where the helicopter waits for you at the destination. If the crew needs to stay overnight, the quote reflects it. Share the full plan in your first message and the number you receive will be final.

Bell 206 or AW139: which helicopter fits your trip?

Choose the Bell 206 for up to 4 passengers with light luggage, and the AW139 for larger groups or when two engines matter to you. That single decision moves the price more than anything else.

The Bell 206 is the efficient choice for executives moving between meetings or a couple heading out for the weekend. The AW139 is a larger, twin-engine aircraft with a VIP cabin for 8. It suits a board traveling together, a family with luggage, or anyone who wants the redundancy of a second engine.

Group size settles most cases. Six passengers fit one AW139 or need two Bell 206 flights, and one AW139 trip is usually simpler to coordinate.
