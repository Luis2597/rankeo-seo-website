How does a round trip to Cancún work?

A round trip is priced as two legs. The return depends on how long you stay. For a short stay, the aircraft and crew can remain in Cancún overnight, which is called a pernocta. For a week on the beach, it usually makes more sense for the jet to fly back to Toluca and return for you on the agreed date.

Each option carries a different cost, and the quote compares both so you can choose. Give us your outbound and return dates in the first message and you will see the full trip priced in one reply.

Which private jet is best for a charter flight to Cancún?

The best aircraft is the smallest one that carries your group and its luggage in comfort. On a 2 hour 15 minute flight, cabin size starts to matter more than on a short hop.

The Learjet 35 is the efficient choice for couples, families and small teams of up to seven. It is the lowest fare on the route and plenty of aircraft for a beach weekend with soft luggage.

The Challenger 605 is the step up for groups of eight to twelve, or for anyone who wants to stand and move around. Its stand-up cabin changes how two hours feel, especially with children or older travelers on board.

The Gulfstream GV makes sense for groups up to sixteen who want to arrive together in a single cabin. It is a long range jet built for nonstop flights to New York and Europe, so on Cancún you choose it for group size and comfort.

Is there a cheaper way to fly private to Cancún?

Yes, if your dates are flexible. Aircraft fly out to Cancún to pick up clients and fly back to Toluca after drop-offs. JETCAB sells those empty legs at up to 30% below the normal fare.
