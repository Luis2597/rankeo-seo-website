Should you fly by helicopter or by private jet?

Use the helicopter for the city and for trips under an hour. Beyond that, the jet wins on time and usually on cost per kilometer. Acapulco takes 1 hour 20 minutes by helicopter and 45 minutes by jet from Toluca, with a base flight from 1,500 USD. Many of our clients do both in one morning: a helicopter from Santa Fe to Toluca, then a private jet charter onward to Cancún, Los Cabos, Miami or Houston. Both legs are coordinated in one quote and the jet waits at the next stand.

Helicopter charter in Mexico City: FAQ

How much does a helicopter charter cost in Mexico City?

A JETCAB helicopter charter starts at 1,500 USD per flight hour on a Bell 206 for 4 passengers and 3,500 USD per flight hour on an AW139 for 8 passengers. The price is per aircraft and includes pilot, fuel and operating permits. Your closed quote arrives in 30 minutes.

Where do helicopter charters depart from in Mexico City?

From authorized helipads in Santa Fe, Polanco and Interlomas, from private helipads at hotels, offices and residences that hold a permit, and from Toluca International Airport, the base of the JETCAB fleet.

How long is the helicopter flight from Toluca airport to Santa Fe or Polanco?

Between 12 and 15 minutes. The same drive takes 40 to 55 minutes outside rush hour, which is why many travelers take the helicopter to reach their private jet in Toluca.

When is a private jet better than a helicopter?

For any trip over about an hour of flight. A jet from Toluca reaches Acapulco in 45 minutes against 1 hour 20 minutes by helicopter, and cities like Monterrey, Cancún or Houston are only practical by jet. Prices start at 1,800 USD per aircraft to Guadalajara.
